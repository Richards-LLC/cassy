So what

The beat is real but lower quality than it looks. $550 K of revenue upside converted to $412 K of operating income; at plan margin it would have been $550 K.
The margin leak is one product line. 1.4 of the 1.6 points came from the hardware bundle, sold at launch pricing 8 % below list.
Q3 depends on one decision. Ending launch pricing on 1 August recovers margin to 61 % and yields the hatched +$180 K; holding it yields roughly +$40 K.

The ask: end hardware-bundle launch pricing on 1 August

Decision needed from the pricing committee by 2026-07-25. The figure shows Q3 operating income variance under each option; the hatched fill is forecast, not measured.
