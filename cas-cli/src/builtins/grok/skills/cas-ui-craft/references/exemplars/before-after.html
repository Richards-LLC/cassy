Fit to argument2The hero shows four numbers in boxes; the reader must read the sentence to learn that one queue moved the other way.
Hierarchy2Title, verdict, and four equal-weight cards compete; the regression is one card among four.

Floor fails: distinctiveness 1, fit 2, hierarchy 2 — not mergeable on a public surface.

Design note · Fit to argumentFour KPI cards are four separate claims of equal weight. The single idea — one queue moved the other way — is buried in the fourth box and in the sentence above it.
Design note · DistinctivenessEverything in this render is a default: the gradient, the 14px radius, the bold 38px title, the uppercase muted labels. Nothing here would tell a reader whose page it is.
Design note · HierarchyTitle, verdict sentence, and four cards all sit above the fold at the same visual weight. The reader has to decide which one is the point.

After — the design language

The verdict sentence in the serif, the one figure whose shape is the claim, a ledger for the audit. Nothing above the fold but the argument.

Concept brief for the after render
Single idea
The bot helped every queue except the one where humans were already fast.
Hero form
Slope chart: four lines fall and one rises; the rising line in the verdict colour is the sentence.
Emotional register
Calm, certain — sandstone hero, serif verdict, no status colour above the fold, the exception named in italic.
Distinctive move
The exception is the only coloured mark on the page, and the only italic word in the verdict.
Deliberately omitted
The KPI card row (four numbers in boxes show no argument), the status column (the sign already says it), the dark gradient.

Design note · Fit to argumentA slope chart has exactly the geometry of the claim: everything falls, one thing rises. No caption is needed to see it.
Design note · HierarchyThe verdict rule is 3px of indigo under a serif sentence. The next thing down is a ledger in 15px mono. There is no third element competing for the first screen.
Design note · CraftLabels sit at both ends of each line, so there is no legend to look up. Status colour appears only in the Δ column, where a sign is also printed.
Design note · DistinctivenessThe "before" and "after" share every number and the same 1,120px container. Everything that differs is a decision the brief names.
Design note · AccessibilityThe SVG carries a title, a description that states the claim in words, and its numbers live in the ledger below it. Dark mode swaps tokens, not markup.
