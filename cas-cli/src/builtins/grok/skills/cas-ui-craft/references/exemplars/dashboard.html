Design note · Hierarchy

A stat strip, not KPI cards. Four figures on one ruled line read as a sentence with commas; four boxes read as four competing headlines and would fight the verdict for the first three seconds.

Design note · Fit to argument

A waffle shows a share as countable units — a reader can literally count the two red squares. A pie of 84/14/2 would have hidden the 2 in a sliver; a stacked bar would have made it a hairline.

Design note · Craft

A ledger, not a data grid: hairline rules only, mono tabular figures right-aligned, a bold sum above a stronger rule, and a source on every row. No zebra stripes — the stripes would compete with the one banded row that matters.

Design note · Accessibility

Status colour appears here and nowhere above: better/worse are real statuses, each printed as a word. On the slope chart Heavy is indigo, not red — it is the argument, not an alarm.
