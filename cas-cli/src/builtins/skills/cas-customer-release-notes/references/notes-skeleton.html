Northwind Planner

Planning a week now takes one screen instead of three.

October 2026 update · prepared for Northwind by the Planner team

What changed

Weekly plan in one view

Was
Store managers opened three pages to see a week's shifts, sales and deliveries.
Now
The weekly plan shows all three side by side, for every store the manager runs.

Exports keep your filters

Was
An export included every store, whatever the screen showed.
Now
An export contains exactly the stores and dates on screen.

Good to know

  • Saved views from before October open in the new weekly plan automatically.
