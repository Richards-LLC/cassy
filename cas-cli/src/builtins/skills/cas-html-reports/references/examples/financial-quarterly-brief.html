Revenue, +$545k favorable

Two enterprise renewals scheduled for Q3 closed in June ($331k), and services utilization ran 6 points above plan ($92k). The remainder ($122k) is volume on existing accounts. None of this is price: list pricing was unchanged in the period.

Cloud infrastructure, +$310k unfavorable

Two thirds ($206k) is the object-storage tier retaining rebuild artifacts that were expected to expire at 30 days and are expiring at 90. The rest tracks the revenue beat and is expected to persist. The retention correction is in flight and is modeled in the Q3 forecast as a $180k reduction — an estimate, based on the current object count and the published per-GB rate, not a measured saving.

Marketing, −$168k favorable

Timing, not saving: a campaign moved to Q3. Treat this line as spent when reading margin.
