### Fixed — Factory

- **Closing a session can no longer lose or misreport a worker's unsaved work** — `worker_status` now shows the worktree's real HEAD and says `[pushed: origin/<branch>]` only when every HEAD commit is on that remote branch; otherwise it says `[NOT PUSHED: N commit(s) not on origin/<branch>]`. A parked tip that differs from HEAD gets its own warning line instead of standing in for HEAD. `shutdown_workers` refusals name each worker's exact remedy. A forced shutdown first saves unpushed commits and uncommitted files to `refs/cas/rescue/<worker>/<time>-<sha>`, pushes that ref to origin, and lists it in the receipt. Worktree removal after shutdown or `/exit` does the same before deleting a branch whose commits are on no remote, and names the ref, so it no longer leaves stranded commits.
