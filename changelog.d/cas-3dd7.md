### Added — Commander

- **Quality sheet** — The command palette opens Quality for the selected conversation's project: the rubric that got worse by the largest factor since the last release leads with its trend line, every worker and supervisor rubric follows per merged delivery (per hour in each row's data), releases whose commits touched a skill, guard or close/merge gate carry a change marker naming it, a release in any row's data opens the merged deliveries and friction behind it with their links, and rubrics without a signal yet show whether they await session scoring or the AI judge. Supervisor tokens are broken down by wake source with the previous release's average.
- **Ledger per supervisor** — `cas ledger report --json` adds `deliveries` and `merged` (distinct tasks whose delivery merged in the window, from the merge record, or unknown when the record does not cover it), `changes` (skills, guards and gates changed between the release tags) and `turns_by_actor` (each supervisor's turns by wake channel), and the hub serves the last five releases plus work in progress read-only at `GET /v1/sessions/{session}/quality`.
