### Fixed — Factory

- **Build disk no longer fills with worker and verifier caches** — Parking a delivery now trims the worker's warm Cargo cache to its compiled dependencies, dropping `incremental/` state and per-commit test executables that previously grew across every task. A new `factory.target_cache_budget_gb` (default 160, 0 disables) bounds all worker `target/debug` caches: when parking exceeds it, Cassy prunes the least recently built idle caches, never one built in the last 30 minutes, holding its build lane, or used by a live process. Verifier walks superseded by a newer walk of the same task are reclaimed without waiting for the task to close, so the newest walk is the only one kept. Park markers for removed worktrees are swept.
