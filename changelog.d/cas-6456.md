### Added — Factory

- **Explicit release-train owner transfer** — Move publication to a named registered supervisor through shared Git authority and atomic target/tag updates. New trains enroll on creation; generic pushes, spawn, child-close advancement and cleanup preserve the fence. Durable PR/release reservations allow verified existing CI continuations to finish while draining, while uncertain requests hold transfer across restart. The draining owner can disarm and reconcile a completed PR arm without a landing. Accept issues a fresh generation; operator-only force/adopt recovery retains external-publication checks. The guarantee covers cooperating enrolled publishers; raw or unsupported writers remain detectable divergence and held recovery.

### Fixed — Factory

- **Preserve filing and tracking during publisher outages** — Prepare a successor and record its publication hold without granting authority. Release tracking remains restart-safe, ordinary epic preparation ignores unrelated backlog corruption and reuses its remote observation, and explicitly unenrolled legacy repairs retain their existing path.
- **Resume an adopted train under its current owner** — Allow the authenticated owner to abort recovery after exact target adoption without creating a successor transfer. Exact authority and generation, provider reconciliation and publisher safety checks still apply.
- **Re-admit a stale train queue after an owner change** — A successor no longer inherits a dead prior owner's merge queue on an enrolled train. The queue stays held until the new owner re-admits its deliveries by listing them; only deliveries still parked are kept, and the earlier CI decision is not carried over.
