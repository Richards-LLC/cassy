### Fixed — Train merge

- **A train merge verifies its queued deliveries at the same time and publishes them in order** — Was: only the delivery at the head of the queue got its candidate and verifier, so each later delivery waited for the one before it to finish a full review before its own started (three deliveries took about 22 minutes while the rest of the queue sat idle with green CI) (GH #2169). Now: each pass of the daemon's train resume prepares the candidate and starts the verifier for every delivery behind the head too, through the same launcher as before; publication still goes one at a time in queue order, and a publish that changes a later delivery's reviewed files re-reviews only that delivery.
