### Changed — Bug intake

- **Find or file** — Was: agents filed a new issue or task for every sighting, so
  one verifier-launch refusal became five issues. Now: every filing path matches
  first. `system action=report_cas_bug` keys on the refusal leading `actual`
  (gate codes plus normalized text), adds evidence and +1 to a matching open
  issue or task, and refuses a report with no key. A finding task create whose
  repro ends with an open task's wrong output is held by G-148, and a QA ledger
  finding like that adds a +1 note to the open task instead of a new follow-up.
  The bug-routing directive, launch contracts, skills, the SessionStart filing
  banners and the staged-report recovery now say "find or file".
