### Changed — Bug intake

- **Find or file** — Was: agents filed a new issue for every sighting, so one
  verifier-launch refusal became five issues. Now: `system action=report_cas_bug`
  reads the verbatim refusal on the first line of `actual`, matches its gate code
  and normalized text against open issues and tasks, and adds the evidence and +1
  to a match instead of filing; the bug-routing directive in AGENTS.md, the launch contracts and the
  worker, supervisor and GitHub-issues skills now say "find or file".
