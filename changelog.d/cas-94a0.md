### Added — Factory

- **/standby parks a supervisor session without ending it** — Was: an idle supervisor with its workers kept using memory, and `/finish` plus `/exit` threw the session away. Now: `/standby` runs the full `finish` flow, then stops every worker without force. A worker with unsaved work refuses and nothing changes; a clean, pushed worker may stop while its task stays in progress. The session records its workers, their tasks and the saved handoff, posts one line and goes quiet, and only the operator and CAS lifecycle events wake it. `factory action=standby status=resume`, or the operator's next message, respawns a replacement for each worker that held tasks and names the handoff to reload. The Commander Standby badge and Resume button follow in a separate change.
