### Changed — Factory guards

- **Most pre-tool guards warn instead of block** — Was: every pre-tool guard refused the call it matched. Now: only the guards for unsaved work, secrets, policy and role tampering, production database writes (a worker's `neonctl` mutation of production and Neon SQL, including through `mcp_execute`), host build caps and Slack through Violet still refuse; every other guard, including the post-release and CI merge checks, records one counted refusal and lets the call run with a one-line warning.
- **Codex hook errors fail open** — Was: any Codex hook error refused the tool call. Now: a hook error runs the call with a recorded refusal after the guards that need no store and the secret-file check pass; an unreadable payload and the capped Cargo route keep refusing.
