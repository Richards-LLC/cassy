### Fixed — Accounts

- **Account ties break on the window that binds, and assumed-empty usage ranks last** — Was: when two accounts had the same headroom on their binding window, the tie was broken by the weekly reset even when the 5-hour window was the one binding. And a window whose reset had already passed was counted as 0% used, so an account with an old 95% snapshot could outrank one with fresh, observed usage. Now: a tie goes to the account whose binding window resets first. Accounts whose usage was observed rank ahead of any whose expired window is only assumed empty; an assumed-empty account is still chosen when nothing observed is eligible.
