### Changed — Factory

- **Review once when only the target moved** — When the target branch moves under an approved delivery and the patch the merge adds is unchanged (same full-context patch-id, or same zero-context patch-id when only nearby context changed), Cassy carries the approval to the new candidate automatically. No verifier runs; the new verdict and a task note record the original verdict, both target commits and the matching patch-id. A target change that alters the delivered patch still gets a fresh review.
- **Verifier deadlines follow progress** — A verification dispatch no longer expires at ten minutes while its verifier is still working. A live verifier heartbeat, a running verifier-lane test or a verifier-test attempt in the last five minutes moves the deadline ten minutes out, up to one hour after the request. Silent dispatches still expire and wake the supervisor as before.
