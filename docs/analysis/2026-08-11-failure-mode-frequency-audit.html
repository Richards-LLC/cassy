3.2 Unit, extraction, and adjudication

The unit is one unique model × harness × session/work-episode × failure class. Repeated evidence in one class/session is one episode; raw occurrences remain separate. Thus three zero-test invocations in one Claude Opus 5 session are 1 episode / 3 events.

The read-only miner inherits model from Claude assistant metadata or Codex turn context; excludes system/developer rows and CAS task-show/sibling-note dumps; and emits one candidate pointer per source/session/class. Accepted evidence is either a unique live guard banner or a concrete supervisor correction, close rejection, blocker/discovery note, or task note naming the actual damage/missing action. Assistant discussion, task descriptions, fixtures, preventive prose, and release-note summaries are rejected. The CSV manifest is the adjudicated source of every number below.

5. Proposed guidance edits — not applied

Approval boundary: this audit changes no rule, skill, or runtime policy. These three edits are proposals for supervisor/operator review.

  1. Add a workspace-path recovery pair. Six denials affected 6 of 86 Claude Fable 5 sessions (6.98%; 0.53% overall). Proposed wording: after a banner, do not retry the same target; route source/build output to the worktree, durable proof to the task artifact root, and ephemeral notes to the harness scratchpad. Treat /dev/null denial as a guard defect to report rather than inventing another host path. The /dev/null and harness-scratchpad rows also justify a runtime-policy follow-up; guidance cannot repair a false boundary.
  2. Make the surface checklist demand a pointer, not an assertion. Four episodes affected 4 of 194 GPT-5.6 Terra sessions (2.06%; 0.35% overall). For every applicable surface, paste the proving file, command, or test; for every N/A, state why. A bare “synced all mirrors” is not close evidence. This builds on cas-641f; it does not rewrite it here.
  3. Add a crossed-message freshness handshake. Four races affected four Codex sessions—2 Sol, 2 Terra; 0.61% across those 652 sessions. After push, MERGE REQUIRED, or a late amendment: drain inbox, re-read the task, test whether the delivered tip is already an ancestor of the target, and check whether the task is already merged/closed. Re-close or stop instead of editing when freshness is already satisfied.

The unscoped-test class had three events in one Opus 5 session. It does not displace the top three because the scoped-test wrapper and passed-count guidance landed afterward. A repeat audit should isolate the post-guard epoch.

6. Threats to validity

  • Lower bound: high precision sacrifices euphemistic or unreported failures.
  • Task-mix confounding: models were routed by depth/cost; some sessions existed to repair incidents.
  • Reporting bias: task-noted failures are visible; silent abandonment is not. Guard denials are mechanically loud.
  • Mixed episode carriers: banners carry session IDs; task-note incidents carry the worker/task episode. Handoffs may undercount unless origin is named.
  • Historical note text: embedded note time, not tasks.updated_at, controls inclusion.
  • Model inheritance: tool results inherit the session’s last model. <synthetic> and unknown are not guessed.
  • Grok absent: v1 makes no cross-harness zero claim for Grok.
  • No causality: the chart is routing/observability evidence, not a benchmark.

Falsifier: a second high-recall adjudication that adds at least seven observed events outside the current top three would overturn the majority finding. Only a post-guidance audit with stable routing can show whether an edit reduced failures.
