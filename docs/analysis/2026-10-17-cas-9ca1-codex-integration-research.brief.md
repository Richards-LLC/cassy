# Brief: Codex integration research, 2026-10-17

## Single idea

Native Codex lifecycle and catalog projections fit inside Cassy authority; transport replacement remains an evidence-gated experiment.

## Hero form

Verdict hero with a compact two-column evidence ledger: demonstrated native primitives face their unresolved authority and transport boundaries at equal visual weight. The paired rows expose why adoption is partial rather than suggesting an unsupported overall score. This is the Research × Practitioner cell.

## Emotional register

Calm, exacting — sandstone field, serif verdict, ruled comparisons, visible uncertainty and plain source lines.

## Distinctive move

The first column says what native evidence establishes; a continuous central rule hands each claim directly to its limiting counter-evidence. The same paired structure returns in the final decision matrix.

## Deliberately omitted

No KPI cards, scores, model-quality ranking or adoption percentage: the corpus is a scope inventory, not a measured performance comparison. No navigation precedes the verdict. No tabs or collapsed source sections hide caveats. No product screenshots imply an executed runtime integration.

## Section forms

Hero: verdict plus evidence ledger. Sources: marginal note/prose. Release coverage: evidence ledger. Lifecycle and subsequent reasoning: prose with marginal headings. Adoption decisions: ruled evidence ledger, because exact owners, proof gaps and migration costs must be audited together. Coverage and confidence: ledger then prose. Provenance: plain commands. No card grid; adjacent technical topics use one continuous narrative form rather than decorative panels.
