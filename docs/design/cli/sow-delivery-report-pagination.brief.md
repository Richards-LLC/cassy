# Brief: continuous SOW print evidence

## Single idea

The client reads full-width comparison evidence through a compact sequence of
A4 pages, without mostly empty sheets between related screens.

## Hero form

Retain the outcome strip and the existing full-width before/after evidence:
each image remains readable, and its caption stays attached while pairs and
sections flow across page boundaries.

## Emotional register

Calm and economical: preserve the existing sample typography, palette and
screen spacing while using tighter print spacing for prose and missing-shot notes.
The print verdict uses the content column, and the outcome strip fits the paper
width without a nearly empty second row.

## Distinctive move

A missing historical screenshot becomes a short honest print note, rather than
an empty image-sized panel; that space carries the next delivered screen.

## Deliberately omitted

No screenshot shrinking, reduced image text, invented historical image or
print-only forced section start. Keep the fictional Northwind sample's palette
because this repairs its template rather than creating a Cassy publication.
Shared capture/inlining/print scripts receive no layout or vocabulary logic.

## Critique

Same-input evidence: four pages become three. Trailing unused printable height
changes from 15.9%, 21.6%, 41.2%, 0.9% to 1.5%, 18.8%, 13.2%; every page is
below 25%. Screenshot width remains 474.75pt and the fixture's smallest 18px
label remains 10.68pt. Text/image bounds determine used page height; blank
pixels inside an image are not counted as a pagination defect.

The independent reader found one small orphan in the missing-history note:
“4.” stood alone. A nonbreaking space now keeps “section 4” together. The
comparison continuing from page 1 to page 2 is clear, with each image and its
source caption intact. Full-width screenshots and body type retain their size;
18pt print section headings and tighter print gaps make the paper sequence compact.

Proof lives in the cas-b0da artifacts: `before.pdf`, `after.pdf`,
`before-measurements.json`, `after-measurements.json`, `pdf-check/receipt.json`
and `visual-qa/visual-qa.json`. Screens are explicitly synthetic local fixtures,
freshly captured through the unchanged capture utility.
