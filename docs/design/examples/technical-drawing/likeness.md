## Cold look (zero-context agent, thumbnail only, verbatim)

1. "A tall, shallow, wall-hung-style cabinet with a pair of doors — reads as a two-door cupboard/cabinet (a bathroom mirror cabinet or a dartboard cabinet shape)."
2. "Upright, standing on its base, drawn in isometric with the front face toward lower-left. Proportions roughly width : height : depth ≈ 1 : 1.3 : 0.3 (taller than wide, quite shallow)."
3. "Visible parts: a rectangular carcass (top, two sides, base), and two front doors meeting at a centre line, each door with a thin vertical strip/frame member along its edge. No hardware, hinges, or interior detail resolvable at this size."

At full size the same agent added: frame-and-panel doors with recessed panels, a distinct centre member where the doors meet, and a separate full-width band below the doors.

Model proportions: 30 : 38 : 10 = 1 : 1.27 : 0.33. The read of 1 : 1.3 : 0.3 is within 3 % on every axis.
