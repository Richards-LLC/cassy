  2. Measure the first five Astra/high heavy deliveries against Sol/high pairs (same brief, both lanes) on send-backs, output tokens, tool calls and minutes to first push from the rollouts. Two deliveries is a sample; five paired is a decision.
  3. Luna/xhigh stays standard everywhere and is the rescue lane for heavy: 272 deliveries across nine projects at $0.69, the lowest send-back rates outside cas-src, and it finished cas-c674 after Sol.
  4. Light = Haiku 4.5 for Slack/release-note and posting chores (22 deliveries, 0 send-backs, $1.46), with a thinking_budget in the recipe instead of low; anything touching code goes to Luna.
  5. Taste = Fable 5.1/high for skill, design and document rewrites (5 of 5 clean); Opus 5/high as taste fallback where it has a record (gabber-studio, pulse-card), not for Woodworking-style long jobs. Supervisor stays Fable 5.1; the registry should say high until a medium day is measured.

Where the rubric fails

1 · Heavy is routed on reputation against the data

Astra has zero worker deliveries in the window and one recorded coordination stall. Sol has one delivery and one send-back. Luna has nineteen merged deliveries and is not in heavy at all.

Heavy is the lane for safety-critical implementation, where "quietly stops" is the worst failure mode.

2 · Silent fallback to the replaced default

Opus 5/high was the built-in supervisor default until 22:00Z on 2026-09-05. It is now the automatic backup for both judgment lanes. A bad auth hour changes the coordinator's behaviour with nobody deciding it.

cas-255e adds a loud receipt. Loud is not approved.

3 · Effort has no stated rationale

Luna xhigh only; Fable medium; Astra medium by recipe but high in heavy; Sol high; Haiku low. A reader cannot tell cost decisions from quality decisions.

4 · Nothing is measured

Every routing change this week came from anecdote: Astra→Fable taste, Fable supervisor, Opus fallbacks, Astra heavy. The actionable-idle metric in 3.17.2 is the first number the rubric has ever had.

5 · The light lane is decorative

Zero uses in the window. Every mechanical chore went to Luna because Haiku is not trusted with builtin marker tests.

6 · Cross-harness fallback inside a lane

Standard falls back from Codex to Claude: different hooks, skill mirror, and account, mid-epic.

7 · Fallback edges declared but disabled

Until cas-255e lands, taste and supervisor carry a fallback that never fires while the comment says "fail closed". A registry that documents one policy and executes another is a review hazard.

Optimizations, in order of leverage

  1. Measure three numbers per lane, per week from data CAS already records: send-backs per delivery (request_changes notes), actionable-idle minutes (3.17.2 metric), assignment-to-first-push minutes (lease start vs first pushed tip). Print them in the generated route table so the rubric and its scorecard are read together.
  2. Promote by trial, not by directive. Keep Sol as heavy primary; make Astra/high the heavy fallback for two weeks; promote only if its send-back rate is at or below Sol's on at least five deliveries.
