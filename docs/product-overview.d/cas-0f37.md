## Core Concepts

A historical factory delivery already contained in its target, with no reusable pre-merge verdict, closes in one live-supervisor `supervisor_override=true` call that cites the containing commit as `commit_receipt`. The content check still runs first and still needs a `reviewed-drop:` reason for any superseded path; that one reason now serves both checks. Cassy records the recovery decision and the commit, creates no pre-merge approval and dispatches no post-merge review. Tier 2 deliveries keep their adversarial review.
