## Core Concepts

### Recorded factory time sinks

The nightly `cas scorecard --since … --until …` view includes per-task phase intervals and completed median/p90 durations, phase tokens, worker/fleet minute buckets, refusal recovery by code, supervisor no-op rates and wake reasons, CI queue/run timing, review rounds and execution minutes, peer admission latency, tokens per shipped bug fix, observed issue closures, and the five largest recorded time sinks. It reads recorded local observations and retained Actions captures, with censored waits, missing attribution, capture gaps, and overlapping cost categories visible.
