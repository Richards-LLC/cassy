## Core Concepts

**Bounded review repairs.** Second pre-merge rejections receive one automatic extra repair round when every
blocking finding concerns documentation, evidence, receipts or unnecessary
complexity and all findings have awkward or cosmetic impact. Cassy uses the
existing structured findings, without interpreting verdict prose. Missing
findings or triage, behavioural or money/data-loss impact, and a third rejection
hold the delivery and ask the supervisor to escalate to the operator. Every
corrected candidate still requires independent approval before publication.
