## Core Concepts

Park evidence publication is limited to declared evidence: top-level files in the task evidence directory, LEDGER.md and the paths its rows cite, and files the close reason cites. Nested files nothing cites are listed in the evidence report as "uncited; not published" and are never uploaded, so a source snapshot or raw capture tree kept beside the proof cannot flood the task with artifact notes or exhaust the close deadline. Routed builds still require their ledger citations, and a cited file that is missing still refuses the park.
