### F1 — Normal — A whole-machine drop raises a transport alarm in the Attention rail (HUB-J12 stages 3, 4, 7; HUB-J8 stage 11)

- **Receipt:** HUB-J12 `receipt.webm` @0:30, @0:35 and @1:14; HUB-J8 @1:27.
- **What the user sees:** next to the plain banner "Lost connection to Atlas · Linux. Reconnecting…", the rail says "1 event needs attention", "Dismiss group" and "×3". The card is headed "Reconnecting to hub" and reads "Stuck dialing atlas.test — node may be offline: Failed to fetch". Later it reads "4 consecutive heartbeats missed" or "Terminal attach failed for atlas.test: network changed". It offers Retry, Dismiss and Details.
- **Why it matters:** HUB-J11's catalog says the rail waits for the banner and only carries a card for a failure that will not retry. HUB-J11 passes because its suite drops a single session. A real network loss is a machine-level drop, and that is when this card appears. It does clear itself once the machine is back.
- **Cause:** `hub-web/src/main.ts:716-724` adds a `hub_disconnected` card on every `backoff`. The text comes from `connection-state.ts:93` and `connection.ts:922-923` / `1146-1147`. `transportFailureNeedsAttention` (`connection-state-view.ts:276`) only covers session transport cards.
- **Fix:** don't add a card while the connection is still retrying. Add one only for a failure that will not retry, and word it like the banner.
- task: cas-7b31

### F2 — Normal — Control is lost after a machine reconnects, against the on-screen promise (HUB-J8 stage 11; HUB-J12 stages 2, 4, 7, 9)

- **Receipt:** HUB-J8 @1:27–1:29 and `J11.png`. During the outage the header promises "Control and interrupts return when it reconnects." Afterwards the header reads OBSERVER / "Take control to enable terminal input, messages, and interrupts."
- **The toast:** "Control released — the hub connection dropped" appears after every machine drop, including in the conversation view.
  - It covers thread text: "After wakin…" and "Not confi…" (HUB-J12 @1:15).
  - It covers the Interrupt button and panel tabs (HUB-J8 @1:27).

- **Cause:** `invalidateMachineLeases` (`main.ts:1992-2008`) clears `held_by_me` and shows the toast. No path takes control back. The promise text is in `connection-state-view.ts:320-321`.
- **Fix:** remember that control was held and take it back once live, unless someone else took it. Otherwise change the promise to "take control again when it's back". Show no toast in the conversation view, and retire the toast when the cause turns out to be a revoked pairing.
- task: cas-7b31

### F3 — Normal — A brief drop throws the reader to the top and brings back "Load earlier" after the start was reached (HUB-J4 stage 4)

### F4 — Normal — Waiting and unconfirmed messages disappear on reload (HUB-J12 stage 8)

- **Receipt:** HUB-J12 `J07.png` compared with @1:24, after the reload. Seven of the user's own messages are gone, including three "Not confirmed" cards and two "Sending…". "While Tailscale is off" also loses its "✓ Delivered".
- **Cause:** sends are not stored anywhere.
  - localStorage and IndexedDB hold no sends (`main.ts:140`, `469`, `501`, `601`, `2812`; `storage.ts:11-12`).
  - Waiting sends live in memory only.
- **Caveat:** the double did receive the unconfirmed ones, and a real hub's history might show them again. The loss of a still-waiting send is inferred from the code. A phone that discards the tab during a network switch hits exactly this case.
- **Fix:** store waiting, unconfirmed and not-sent messages per session with their `client_ref`, restore them on load, and send waiting ones exactly once when live.
- task: cas-e7b1
