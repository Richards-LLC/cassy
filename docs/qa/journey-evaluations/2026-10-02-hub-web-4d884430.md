### F33 — Normal — NEW — The desktop End session confirmation puts its destructive button under the pointer; a double-click ends the session (HUB-J14)

- **Journey/stage:** HUB-J14, End a stale session (desktop grouped list).
  HUB-J16's phone layout is not affected.
- **Receipts:**
  - `eval/frames/HUB-J14/f039.png` (@00:19.0): the click lands on the row's
    "End session" at about (128, 584).
  - `f040.png` (@00:19.5): the confirmation's red "End session" occupies
    about x 91–180, y 565–604, drawn hovered under the same pointer.
  - `trace-actions.txt` action 127 is the click. The confirmation exists 63 ms
    later (actions 128–131).
- **Experience:** the F19 fix made the confirmation instant. Because it lands
  under the cursor, a double-click, or an impatient second click, confirms
  ending the supervisor and its workers.
- **Fix:**
  - Place Cancel, which already has focus, where the trigger was, and move the
    destructive button away from that point.
  - Or ignore activation for about 400 ms after the confirmation appears.
  - Add a journey assertion that a `dblclick` on End session leaves the row.
- task: cas-f60a. Steps 1.
