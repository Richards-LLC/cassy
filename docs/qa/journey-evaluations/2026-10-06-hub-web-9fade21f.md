**No Blocking finding. The 3.48 cut may proceed on this dist. Every journey reaches its goal, and no core journey scores a 2.**

What the evidence shows:

- **Glass keeps the markup.** Across every receipt, the final accessibility trees match 3.47.1. The only differences are the build id, ports, relative times, and the mic button settling to "Start listening" sooner.

The supervisor folded all of them into cas-205e, which merged before this run, and the receipts at 9fade21f show each one resolved (H01–H06, H12, H13):

  cas-205e passed independent QA in two rounds. Round 1 rejected an installations-sheet regression, and round 2 approved the fix at 3f7a91a4.
- **3.47.1's Normal batch (cas-d043, G01–G15) persists unchanged.** No delivery in this release targeted it.
