
**Only reply:**

```text
• *Commander polish* — Was: the 2026-09-23 journey evaluation logged clipping, overlap, focus-to-body, re-colouring and forced-colour gaps. → Now: all fixed, each with independent QA and a journey stage; the combined epic walk scored 7/8 cells, and the eighth (outage contrast) is fixed too (PR #1010).

• *QA and close gates* — Was: a rejected QA round sometimes opened no next round, and a close after a handoff measured a branch that didn't exist. → Now: round N+1 always opens, and close measures the branch holding the commits (PR #1010).

• *Store and guards* — Was: task writes failed with "database is locked" under load, and a worker's Neon SQL could write to production. → Now: writes take BEGIN IMMEDIATE with retry, and production-branch writes from workers are denied (PR #1010).

• *Test hygiene* — Was: journeys could attach to another checkout's server, and the systemd restart test killed itself on hosts running a hub. → Now: journeys use per-checkout ports and refuse a taken port, and the test uses a disposable child PID (PRs #1010, #1012).
```
