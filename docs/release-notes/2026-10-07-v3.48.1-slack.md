## User thread

**Top-level:**

```text
*Live on production — User — Cassy v3.48.1*
```

**Reply (Was → Now):**

```text
*Commander: held messages go out, plus a polish pass*

*Sending*

• *Held messages go out after reconnect* — Was: if the machine came back mid-send, a held message could sit unsent with nothing trying it again. → Now: it goes out promptly once Commander reconnects, exactly once.

• *No Retry while another tab is sending* — Was: with two tabs open, "Retry" could flash in one tab while the other was still sending, inviting a double send. → Now: both tabs wait for that send to confirm, and a new reply no longer cuts the wait short.

*Polish*

• *Long machine names read in full on phones* — Was: a long machine name was cut off beside its codename. → Now: the name gets its own row and reads whole, and the codename steps aside when space runs out.

• *Stop result stays in view* — Was: after Stop, the result line could scroll out of sight. → Now: it stays on screen beside the next row, and the confirmation says the task goes back to Open.

• *Revoking says what happened* — Was: revoking this browser's own access dropped you on a silent first screen. → Now: Commander says the access was revoked and puts you on Pair a machine.

• *Plain words for browser problems* — Was: a blocked permission or an unsupported browser read "Can't reach". → Now: it reads "Blocked by browser" or "Browser can't connect".

• *Keyboard reaches Interrupt quickly* — Was: Interrupt was about a dozen Tab presses from search. → Now: "Skip to the conversation" gets you there in three keys, with a ring at every stop.

• *Pinned bar readable on hover in dark* — Was: hovering the pinned question bar in dark turned it navy under dark text. → Now: it keeps its amber fill and only lifts.

• *Assign names its task* — Was: the Assign picker showed only the task's ID. → Now: it names the task by its title.

• *Landscape refusals stay in view* — Was: on a phone held sideways, a refusal in Tasks & progress landed below the fold. → Now: it rides the bottom edge of the sheet.

• *Undo dismiss says what happens* — Was: dismissing the phone Undo offer gave no hint where Undo went. → Now: the dismiss button says Undo stays in Tasks & progress, and it does.

• *Lighter pairing buttons* — Was: in light, the pairing dialog's button bar read as a whiter slab. → Now: it is part of the sheet and frosts only while fields scroll beneath it.

• *Answers minutes apart get their own time* — Was: replies minutes apart merged under one time. → Now: a five-minute gap starts a new group with its own time, and reload keeps the grouping.

• *Clearer during an outage* — Was: a reload mid-outage read "0 conversations" and the banner covered the thread's top. → Now: it reads "Conversations not loaded yet" and the banner sits above the thread.

• *Who is in control* — Was: you learned another device held control only by trying to take it. → Now: the header names that device, and a refusal says how to get past it.

• *Plainer pairing* — Was: the admin permission showed its raw code and every browser had the same default name. → Now: it reads "See and revoke other browsers on" the machine, and a browser is named like "Chrome on Linux".

• *Release effort* — 2 manual interventions were needed to publish this release.
```

## Dev thread

**Top-level:**

```text
*Live on production — Dev — Cassy v3.48.1*
Was: a Live wake mid-settlement stranded held sends, and peer tabs offered Retry early. → Now: wakes coalesce into one rerun, and peer receipt deadlines hold.
```

**Reply (Was → Now):**

```text
*Commander send fixes, polish and journey tooling*

• *Held-send wake coalescing* — Was: `HeldSendFlushes` returned early while a batch held the key, so a Live callback during a definite no-write settlement was lost and the message stalled. → Now: a mid-batch call marks the batch to rerun with the latest work, the journal still gates every write, and uncertain or revoked claims never replay.

• *Peer receipt deadlines* — Was: a tab projecting another tab's durable `sending` claim marked it unconfirmed at once and showed Retry. → Now: it keeps the claim's original 15 s receipt clock, and the journal refuses to re-hold an active peer claim even from a stale Retry.

• *Peer clocks ignore local replies* — Was: a reply landing in one tab applied the 5 s reply grace to a peer's send. → Now: restored projections keep the original timeout, a lost dispatch claim reads durable state instead of unconfirming, and history settles restored sends as one row.

• *Phone header and Stop result* — Was: the phone identity shared a row with the actions, so names truncated beside the codename. → Now: identity has its own row, the machine never shrinks while the codename shows, and Stop scrolls its result into view.

• *Browser-side status* — Was: blocked permission and unsupported browser fell through to "Can't reach", and one session's retry set the machine footer to Reconnecting. → Now: `BROWSER_BLOCKED` and `BROWSER_UNSUPPORTED` labels reach header, row, footer and launch sheet, and the footer ignores a sibling retry.

• *Keyboard, control and pairing copy* — Was: Interrupt was 12 Tabs from search, control holders were hidden, and pairing showed raw scope ids. → Now: a skip link, a header control line, plain admin consent, a `browserLabel` default name and `aria-labelledby` on every pair dialog.

• *Thread time groups and kept replies* — Was: turns minutes apart merged under one time, and a journal-restored reply's stored time posed as a machine stamp. → Now: `GROUP_SPLIT_MS` splits on a five-minute gap, and kept replies take the machine stamp once history arrives.

• *Glass details* — Was: the pinned bar lost its amber on hover in dark, the rail sliced rows at its corners, and the pair action bar frosted into a slab. → Now: hover keeps the ask fill, rail edges fade only with rows beyond, and the bar frosts only over scrolled fields.

• *Fleet sheet details* — Was: the Assign picker showed only an id, a landscape refusal fell below the fold, and dismissing Undo was ambiguous. → Now: the picker names the task title, the notice is sticky at the sheet's bottom, and the dismiss label says Undo stays.

• *Journey selection at the reviewed head* — Was: the close gate and `journey-receipt.py` ran `journeys-for-diff.py` from the shared checkout, whose older copy could ignore `CAS_JOURNEYS_HEAD`. → Now: both run the selector blob from the reviewed head over stdin and refuse proof if it is missing or broken.

Published archives: `cas-x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu.tar.gz` (SHA-256 `7ff34b5843e5e424951541bfff3dfa9568c150e40ea1d50c464df14f27444043`) and `cas-aarch64-apple-darwin.tar.gz` (SHA-256 `834be230088a6197eb3ddc0ba62f717d3901fa2bacd3fc985dd38f0a39d0dcee`), both built by CI regardless of the tagger's host.
```
