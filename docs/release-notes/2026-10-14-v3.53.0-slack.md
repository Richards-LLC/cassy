
**Reply (Was → Now):**

```text
*Release pipeline*

• *Parallel gate* — Was: about 30 release-gate rows ran sequentially (29 min on 3.51.1). → Now: rows declare `cpu`, `mem=`, `excl=`, `after=` and `writes-tree`, run concurrently under the assembly-proof memory budget with private scratch, and keep canonical receipt order. `CAS_RELEASE_GATE_PARALLEL=1` restores serial.

• *Shift left* — Was: second-scale mistakes cost a CI or merge-queue round. → Now: `--fast-rows` covers next-version literals, `CARGO_BIN_EXE_*`, call shapes and release-notes drafts, prints `FIX` lines, and runs in a factory pre-push hook. `test-release-gate.sh` went from 2165 s to about 115 s.
```
