## User thread

**Top-level:**

```text
*Live on production — User — Cassy v3.61.0*
Was: supervisors worked in separate silos and missed their own reminders. → Now: they can reach each other across projects, and every reminder wakes them once.
```

**Reply (Was → Now):**

```text
• *Supervisors across projects* — Was: a supervisor could only message supervisors in the same project. → Now: any of your own live supervisors, on any project or machine, can be listed with `cas peers --all` and messaged; other people's supervisors stay invisible. Cross-project delivery needs the matching Cloud update.

• *Who else is here* — Was: after a restart, a supervisor didn't know another one was working on the same repo. → Now: it sees each live peer, its machine and its work as soon as it starts, gets an "about to merge" note before a peer merges into a shared branch, and is warned when two branches change the same file.

• *Quieter, clearer inbox* — Was: messages from other supervisors got lost among system notices, reminders could sit unread for minutes, and false "stalled" alarms piled up. → Now: messages from other supervisors and from you come first, each reminder wakes the supervisor once, and alarms fire only when something is really waiting.

• *Tidier backlog* — Was: finished work and stale tasks stayed open unnoticed. → Now: `cas doctor` and supervisor startup point them out with evidence, and finished epics close without manual steps.

• *Your memories are safe when projects merge* — Was: merging two projects could hide a memory that happened to share an id with another. → Now: new memories get unique ids, and earlier clashes are recovered.

• *Projects keep syncing* — Was: a project nobody had open on a machine stopped syncing, and team projects could stop pulling. → Now: the Commander hub syncs idle projects in the background, and team projects sync in team scope.

• *One Claude setup for every account* — Was: commands and skills could disappear when you switched Claude accounts. → Now: `cas accounts link` shares everything but the login across accounts (preview it with `--dry-run`, undo it with the printed restore script).

• *Commander* — Was: a reload while a machine was offline showed no conversations. → Now: your last conversation appears at once, and a machine that reports its presence shows when it goes offline and comes back.

• *Release reports you can read* — Was: reports said "0 issues closed" and used internal jargon. → Now: they lead with what shipped, in plain words, with Eastern times.

Install: `cas update`, or download v3.61.0 for Linux x86_64 (SHA-256 `{{LINUX_SHA256}}`) or macOS ARM64 (SHA-256 `{{MACOS_SHA256}}`) from <https://github.com/Richards-LLC/cassy/releases/tag/v3.61.0|the release>.
```

## Dev thread

**Top-level:**

```text
*Live on production — Dev — Cassy v3.61.0*
Was: peer coordination stopped at the repo boundary and wakes were noisy. → Now: user-scoped peer messaging, one inline turn per event, and stricter close and CI gates.
```

**Reply (Was → Now):**

```text
• *Peer scope* — Was: discovery and `PeerSend` were filtered by repo. → Now: `cas peers --all` and `coordination peers scope=all` list the caller's own live supervisors across projects and machines; targets resolve by name, name@machine or project; cross-user is refused. Live cross-project delivery waits on the Cloud deploy.

• *Shared-target helpers* — Was: no peer signal before a merge. → Now: `worktree_merge` sends one best-effort about-to-merge note per tip, and `cas peers` plus SessionStart warn on same-file overlap; no locks, waits or CI stage. SessionStart also lists live same-repo peers with owner, and `peers.same_repo_scope = own` filters them.

• *Supervisor wakes* — Was: wake pointer, body and `[redelivery]` replays per event; fired `ci_completed` reminders gated as `gated_not_ready`; self reminders raised `supervisor_unread`. → Now: bounded bodies inline once, reminders wake once with transport-only cadence, self reminders park silently, peer and operator mail leads the block, and notices about ended recipients fold into a digest row.

• *False alarms* — Was: stalled and unread notices for workers that had acted; abandoned merge-request wakes reported "(unknown task) moved on"; MERGE REQUIRED relays fired on pending CI and stale origin. → Now: implicit acknowledgement from later outbound activity (workers only), actionable messages survive wake exhaustion, and relays wait for the tip's CI against a freshly fetched target.

• *Close and epic gates* — Was: local-only anchors, a pre-close check against the worktree's next branch, merge-only deliveries unattributed, proofs claimed without receipts, auto-unblock releasing non-dependency holds, `adopt_branch` refused for isolated workers. → Now: anchors must be on origin, each task is measured on its own branch, merge-only deliveries attribute, future proofs need citations, holds are structured, and isolated workers adopt into their managed worktree. Dead-owner epics are adopted on evidence, default-branch containment counts, and repos without a merge queue require green CI on the exact default-branch SHA (GH #1243, #1244).

• *Shell guard* — Was: `)` from a `$(…)` reset command position, and `cd`, `sh -c` and same-command links escaped path tracking. → Now: one statement splitter keeps argument position, the effective cwd is carried, and links are judged at their real destination, all fail-closed.

• *Sync* — Was: per-checkout memory ids collided in alias folds (GH #1240); team-linked daemons pushed personal rows and never pulled team scope; purge freshness read the personal watermark; idle stores never synced. → Now: random 128-bit ids with deterministic recovery and Cloud's rekey rule v1, team-scope-only daemon sync, scope-aware purge freshness, and hub idle sync under a per-store lease.

• *Health and hygiene* — Was: nothing measured comms regressions or stale backlog. → Now: five communication SLOs in doctor, shutdown and the release gate, and four backlog-hygiene detectors (delivered-but-open, finished-but-open epics, idle, cited code gone) in doctor and supervisor SessionStart.

• *Factory runtime* — Was: Claude `/clear` dropped registration; standalone-daemon Claude workers lacked a cas_root; macOS relaunch dropped resolved specs; Codex PreToolUse could fail open under load; worker status could hang on `gh`. → Now: all fixed. Claude agents get role session names and cas-only strict MCP by default (`factory.claude.worker_mcp = "inherit"` opts out), plus `remote_control` and `fallback_model` keys; `factory.scratch_root` relocates worker targets, build cache, merge-sweep and assembly caches, and release_env accepts it across devices.

• *Accounts* — Was: Claude account folders drifted. → Now: `cas accounts link [--dry-run]` and `cas accounts drift` share everything but login files, with a printed restore script.

• *Commander* — Was: conversations depended on the pane grid; an unreachable machine showed zero conversations. → Now: conversations mount without the grid and keep the thread on reconnect, the last conversation renders from a cached catalog, and the opted-in hub presence reporter feeds machine-offline notices; the cloud inbox journey runs against the deployed cloud.

• *CI and releases* — Was: web-only lanes reran Rust, QA replayed journeys, the journey shards installed fonts, and one 403 failed the stale-run watchdog. → Now: first-push scope uses the epic merge base, journeys run once, the font step is gone, and the watchdog relays only CI verdicts. Release reports count issues after closure, group by area and print Eastern 12-hour times.

• *Groundwork, inactive* — Was: no client for shared work claims. → Now: a request journal and factory claim, status, release, transfer and accept actions exist, inert until the Cloud and Violet services ship.

• *Tests* — Was: flakes in a shutdown drain, a torn inbox read (inbox writes are now atomic under a lock), random-id ordering and env-leaking update tests. → Now: deterministic and isolated. Skills now carry one "fewest tests" principle, and merge requests show test, source and docs line counts.

Validation: merge-queue proof of the release tree. Published archives: `cas-x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu.tar.gz` (SHA-256 `{{LINUX_SHA256}}`) and `cas-aarch64-apple-darwin.tar.gz` (SHA-256 `{{MACOS_SHA256}}`), both built in CI.
```
