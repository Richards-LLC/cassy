## User thread

Top-level:

```text
*Live on production — User — Cassy v3.96.0*
Was: messages and replies could stall or lose their place. → Now: they reach the right conversation and recover through connection trouble.
```

Reply (Was → Now):

```text
*Messages and replies*

• *Send without the wait* — Was: Background activity could hold a message even while a conversation was ready. → Now: Your message reaches the conversation like typing in its terminal, whether it is idle or busy.

• *Keep words through setup* — Was: A sign-in or welcome screen could consume a message shown as delivered. → Now: The original words wait for a text prompt, and confirmation dialogs keep their input protection.

• *Answers keep their place* — Was: Early messages or a restarted conversation could lose an answer or show it in the wrong place. → Now: Claude and Codex replies stay with the original conversation and device.

• *One answer per turn* — Was: Background notices could mix with a reply or confuse its delivery state. → Now: Status updates stay separate, each answer appears once, and consumed messages stay marked Delivered.

*Connections and display*

• *Recover after reboot* — Was: A temporary Tailscale outage could leave the remote address pointing at a stopped service. → Now: The hub retries in the background and restores the address when Tailscale returns.

• *Keep local access* — Was: Missing Tailscale or unfinished setup could stop the local hub too. → Now: Automatic mode keeps local access available with a warning while the remote connection is unavailable.

• *Protect your other services* — Was: A failed hub start could remove a stopped service you had put on the same remote port. → Now: It leaves your service alone and removes only an exactly recorded Cassy target.

• *Phone actions stay visible* — Was: A delivery warning could push header buttons off the screen. → Now: The buttons stay in view, including the Raw output icon.

*Feedback and release scope*

• *Feedback says what was measured* — Was: Release feedback could include lifetime totals or make an empty result sound unknown. → Now: It excludes unrelated totals and clearly says when no feedback was recorded.

• *Older conversations still stay listed* — Was: This release was going to hide stopped conversations and add recovery for their unsent drafts. → Now: That change is deferred; non-running sessions remain listed until the next release.

Running Cassy sessions need a restart to pick up instant delivery.

Update with `cas update`, or choose <https://github.com/Richards-LLC/cassy/releases/tag/v3.96.0|the Linux x86_64 or macOS ARM64 downloads>.
```

## Dev thread

Top-level:

```text
*Live on production — Dev — Cassy v3.96.0*
Was: delivery, reply ownership and route recovery could drift. → Now: native input, durable reply binding and background publication recover together.
```

Reply (Was → Now):

```text
*Delivery and reply identity*

• *Native input delivery* — Was: Fresh MCP heartbeats could gate verified Commander sends. → Now: Idle and working prompts receive terminal-style input, while recipient reads and acknowledgements update connected devices.

• *Prompt readiness* — Was: Claude setup pages or native confirmation states could consume queued input. → Now: Original messages wait for a ready text prompt; non-Claude dialogs and pending tools keep their guards.

• *Claude reply binding* — Was: Startup and same-name replacement could detach replies from their original send or paired device. → Now: Native answer mirroring retains both bindings and emits one answer per turn.

• *Codex reply binding* — Was: Shared-project, framed and long-rollout answers could use the wrong session or repeatedly rescan history. → Now: Streamed ownership follows the correct rollout and unresolved watches back off.

• *Receipt reconciliation* — Was: Background notices could replace an operator turn and old finalized Queued receipts could stop watching. → Now: Standalone status stays separate, consumed sends retain Delivered and old receipts keep a slower delivery watch.

*Transport and safety*

• *Auto publication recovery* — Was: Exhausted Tailscale retries could stop local access or require a restart. → Now: Auto services retain loopback, retry publication in the background and update live route status when it succeeds. Required mode remains strict.

• *Exact Serve ownership* — Was: A stale receipt could treat a different dead loopback target as owned. → Now: Cleanup and replacement require an exact receipt, runtime-record or captured prior target; conflicts offer a port-specific operator remedy.

• *Unavailable Tailscale* — Was: Missing CLI or incomplete setup could fail an Auto service. → Now: A sanitized warning preserves loopback access; retained target evidence survives failed starts.

• *Authenticated runtime identity* — Was: Nested or read-only calls could borrow a live owner's identity and disrupt its communication. → Now: Ownership checks protect reads, writes and lifecycle cleanup, including exited-PID and hostname recovery.

*Display, feedback and validation*

• *Phone header bounds* — Was: A warning could overflow the action group. → Now: Actions stay inside the viewport and Raw output retains its icon.

• *Scoped release feedback* — Was: Lifetime retrieval snapshots could be dated as new release evidence. → Now: Reports exclude unattributed totals, disclose exclusions and distinguish no feedback from excluded measurements.

• *Candidate source identity* — Was: Generated web assets could dirty a source-only candidate. → Now: Verified generated assets preserve source identity, while unrelated edits and served-asset mismatches still fail.

• *Recovery and framing tests* — Was: Fixtures expected manual restart, obsolete message framing or unverified process reuse. → Now: They assert automatic publication recovery, framed Codex reply binding and owned-process fingerprints.

• *Explicit scope deferral* — Was: The proposed release removed stopped sessions and changed unsent-draft recovery. → Now: Both changes are reverted here; non-running sessions remain listed until the next release.
```
