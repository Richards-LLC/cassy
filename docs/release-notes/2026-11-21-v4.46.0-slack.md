```text

```

Reply (Was → Now):

```text
*Work closes, trains cut, evidence stays*

• *Finished work parks instead of bouncing* — Was: a session's report-only close on a release train was refused outright, and a failed evidence upload could block an approved delivery. → Now: both park for review with the evidence they need.

• *One next train after a cut* — Was: cutting a train while new work waited could open two next trains and split the work. → Now: the cut reuses the train that's already open.

• *Moving work between trains is safe* — Was: a failure partway through a move could leave a task with no train. → Now: the move happens in one step or not at all.

• *Failed tests keep their evidence* — Was: a failed browser or unit test run deleted the reports it had just pointed to. → Now: the reports stay in the task's evidence folder, and the error names where.

• *Fewer false alarms* — Was: shipped trains kept asking to be cut, a live Mac session showed up as abandoned work, and the CI watchdog cancelled runs that were just waiting their turn. → Now: none of those happen.

• *Steadier workspace* — Was: back-to-back session starts could refuse a working Codex account, a busy database could drop a session reset, and a Slack reply after an idle spell could fail. → Now: each one retries or recovers on its own.
```
