What you can do now

02 / 06
01

Releases that do not wait

Combined as you go

Wasfinished pieces were only combined at release time, and every clash surfaced then.

Nowevery finished piece is combined with everything else the moment it lands, and clashes are reported to the person who owns them right away.

Proof that covers the change

Wasa change could pass its own checks and still break a neighbour.

Nowa change cannot be handed in until the checks that exercise it have run.

Small fixes move fast

Wasa one-line fix waited on the same long check as a big change.

Nowsmall, verified fixes are admitted immediately.

Fix rounds in parallel

Waseach failure was written up and fixed one after another.

Nowfailures are grouped and handed out at once.

One short gate

Wasthe final release check redid work already proven.

Nowproven results are reused and only what changed is rechecked.

Under the hood

03 / 06
01

Integration

Rolling integration branch

Wasepics were merged one by one at release time.

Noweach epic merge re-merges main plus every open epic into integration/<project>, runs the workspace sweep detached, and posts conflict pairs/files or failing targets to the owning supervisors and as epic notes.

Proof surface mapping

Wascheck-scoped-test-surface.sh mapped files to tests that mention them; --lib-only proofs passed.

Nowchanged modules require the integration binaries that exercise them plus class guardrails (skills → builtin_*, tests/ → archive portability, hooks → hook_schema); close refuses with the exact command.

Fast merge admission

Wasevery lane waited 10–25 min on Scoped Validation.

Nowrelease-train.sh --check-lane accepts a supervisor --proof receipt on the exact tip for small deltas, and a Scoped Validation (fast) tier compiles plus mapped targets; the policy is pinned by test-ci-test-tiers.sh.

Sweep → tasks

Wassweep logs were read by hand into one task per round.

Nowfailures group by binary + panic site into sweep-tasks.json; coordination action=sweep_tasks previews and accepts, creating tasks and spawning workers idempotently.

Gate reuse from sweep receipts

Wasthe gate reran nextest/archive rows already proven by the assembly sweep.

Nowthe integration sweep writes row-cache-v2 receipts; --gate --reuse accepts them for the exact tip with matching checkout, inputs, environment and toolchain under 24 h, and logs the source SHA.
