## Under the hood

### Visibility and shell checks

#### Paired-machine controls

Was: saved endpoints lacked a management view.

Now: connection, runtime and last-seen details accompany confirmed browser-local removal and distinct Cloud/Commander branding.

#### Conversation freshness

Was: cached sessions could outlive their reachability or active membership.

Now: heartbeat refresh and freshness expiry govern the default catalog, preserving recovery views and pending work.

#### Session-scoped counts

Was: name deduplication could obscure cross-session records.

Now: status partitions registered membership by session before deduplication and labels its scope.

#### Structural shell guards

Was: literal script content could look like an executable write.

Now: heredocs, comments and quoted arguments are parsed without relaxing real write or formatter restrictions.

#### Reporting destinations

Was: missing project issue configuration or an implicit home scan could misroute reporting.

Now: the Cassy repository fallback and resolved hygiene artifact root are explicit.

### Proof and recovery

#### Merge-content proof

Was: merge topology could obscure retained edits.

Now: verification preserves valid content evidence while rejecting merges that drop delivered changes.

#### Declared proof destination

Was: checkout HEAD or a main-only comparison could determine evidence scope.

Now: post-merge proof and scoped receipts resolve the declared WorkTarget branch.

#### Valid scoped tests

Was: nested roots, repeated `--lib` arguments or older receipts could distort validation.

Now: target discovery, multi-module arguments and latest-receipt selection agree.

#### Stable integration inputs

Was: ownership and delivery-target confusion could omit live source branches.

Now: the shared task set resolves live open coordination branches and excludes terminal or missing branches.

#### Stable failure evidence

Was: reruns could repeat notices for unchanged main failures or invent a classification.

Now: normalized base-failure evidence deduplicates notices, and unknown assertions stay unclassified.

#### Recipient-aware commands

Was: recovery hints could inherit the daemon's tool prefix.

Now: request-local callers and persisted role-specific CLI evidence select Claude, Codex, Grok or OpenCode aliases.

#### Resumable relay delivery

Was: recovery prose was bound too early and partial retries could duplicate work.

Now: neutral facts render for the recipient at delivery, with recorded outbox progress.

#### Safe rejection context

Was: quotes or newlines could break the suggested close reason.

Now: a stable audit argument accompanies the unchanged rejection context.

#### Authenticated recovery wakes

Was: `merged_close_blocked` and `pr_lane_failed` were missing from the accepted daemon kinds.

Now: valid daemon envelopes can wake processing, while forged or unattributed copies remain rejected.

### Guidance and publication

#### Consistent role contracts

Was: startup, acceptance and close instructions could conflict.

Now: task-start acceptance, close ownership and trusted wake guidance align, with Codex-specific startup and retained recall coverage within prompt budgets.

#### MechaCassy-only publication

Was: release guidance permitted a personal Slack connector fallback.

Now: mirrored instructions and project runbooks require the same authenticated hub, including direct access, with partial-receipt and uncertain-write safeguards intact.
