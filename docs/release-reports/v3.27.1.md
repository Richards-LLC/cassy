## What you can do now

### Only reply:

#### Answers arrive

Was: Cassy could address its reply to your name instead of the conversation, and it vanished while still reporting "sent". →

Now: replies to your name reach you, and a reply to nowhere is refused on the spot.

#### Typing on the phone

Was: opening the keyboard pushed the header and the latest messages off the top of the screen. →

Now: header, latest message and the box you are typing in all stay in view.

#### Hub comes back after updates

Was: updating Cassy could leave your hub running the old version, outside its service. →

Now: the update restarts the hub through its service on the new version, at the same address.


## Under the hood

### Only reply:

#### Reply routing

Was: message accepted any target string; a `commander:…` reply had no verified-device stamp, so `peek_operator_replies` never selected it and the daemon abandoned it 15 min later. →

Now: `commander:<label>` resolves the newest verified Commander row (in_reply_to + device), row-source prefixes and empty targets are refused before enqueue, queue-before-register for plain names is unchanged, and Commander rows are framed with the exact reply command in inbox, hook and daemon renderers.

#### Keyboard viewport

Was: viewport meta without `interactive-widget`, 100dvh shell scrolled under the soft keyboard. →

Now: `interactive-widget=resizes-content`, `--keyboard-viewport-height` from visualViewport as fallback, `followTail()` on composer focus; pebble-qa keyboard receipts at 390x544 on both paths.

#### Hub service restart

Was: update relaunched a detached `cas hub serve --launched-by update` beside an inactive unit and later left the old binary running. →

Now: with cas-hub.service installed, restart goes through `systemctl --user restart`, the outcome records old→new version, the unit keeps `--tailscale-serve`, and status/doctor flag an inactive unit with a detached lock holder; systemctl is injected via `CAS_HUB_SYSTEMCTL` in tests.
