## What you can do now

### Only reply:

#### History on open

Was: the hub only remembered messages while the page stayed open; a fresh open or a hub restart showed nothing. →

Now: opening a conversation brings back your messages and Cassy's replies for that machine, with a control to load earlier ones.

#### Only your conversation

Was: n/a. →

Now: history is limited to messages sent from your paired device and replies addressed to it; terminal output is never shown.

#### Needs both sides updated

Was: n/a. →

Now: a machine still on an older version keeps the previous behaviour until it updates.


## Under the hood

### Only reply:

#### Store

Was: no history read. →

Now: `PromptQueueStore::conversation_history(session, device, before, limit)` returns verified Commander sends for that device plus operator-lane replies addressed to it or to the wildcard recipient, ordered with an explicit cursor.

#### Protocol

Was: n/a. →

Now: a history request/response pair on the client/daemon protocol, request-correlated; the hub enforces the authenticated device id before dispatch and gates the page by request id.

#### hub-web

Was: hydration impossible. →

Now: `ConversationHistory` hydrates from the page before live frames, dedupes by notification id and client ref, renders through the Pebble thread model, and pages with "load earlier"; dist rebuilt once.
