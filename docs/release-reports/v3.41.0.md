## What you can do now

### Only reply (user)

#### Finished work closes

Was: a task whose work was already shipped could stay stuck as "waiting to merge" if a later change reworded or extended any of its lines. →

Now: Cassy recognises the later change and lets the task close, and it still refuses when work was actually lost.

#### Clearer automatic checks

Was: the automatic check after each merge failed every time on a machine that had not been told where to keep its scratch copy. →

Now: it says the setting is missing and names it, and runs normally once it is set.

#### Better testing guidance

Was: the testing guide listed rules without examples. →

Now: it includes worked good and bad examples and a guide to what should and should not be faked in a test.


## Under the hood

### Only reply (dev)

#### Line-ownership tracing

Was: `anchored_delivery_content_gate` reported DELIVERY CONTENT DROPPED when a later commit modified or extended a delivered line, and UNVERIFIABLE when the content lived in a merge resolution. →

Now: delivered lines are carried through the descendant DAG. Ordinary replacements, task-owned novel resolutions (remerge-proven), additive identifier-list unions and task-internal draft evolution are credited and recorded; reverts, stale-superset merges, plain deletions and foreign novel resolutions still reject, and git errors fail closed.

#### Reviewed-drop exit

Was: `supervisor_override` could not pass the content gate, and narrative receipts were rejected. →

Now: `reason="reviewed-drop: <SHA>[,<SHA>...] -- <why>"` resolves full commits, requires strict anchor descent, target reachability, first-parent touch of every dropped path, and records all of it.

#### Sweep scratch root

Was: daemon rolling integration inherited no `CAS_RELEASE_GATE_HOME_DIR`, so the assembly proof refused `/var/tmp` on every sweep. →

Now: `factory.release_gate_home_dir` is passed to the assembly child; unset reports `sweep_not_configured` with no attribution probe.

#### cas-tdd references

Was: `references/tests.md` and `mocking.md` had few repo-specific examples. →

Now: worked Rust store/Git fixture, `TestEnvGuard` and hub-web Vitest pairs, plus a boundary table.
