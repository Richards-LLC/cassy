## What you can do now

### Only reply (user)

#### A steady connection picture

Was: a machine that dropped could show a stale "Reconnecting to hub" card, hand back the wrong words after a refused pairing, or stick on Reconnecting. →

Now: one word, "Unsteady", covers a shaky machine, messages wait until it is steady, control comes back with the machine, and every card names the machine.

#### Nothing you typed is lost on reload

Was: a reload threw away drafts and any message that was waiting or unconfirmed. →

Now: drafts and those messages survive, each in its own state, a waiting one goes out exactly once, and they show at once even during an outage.

#### Your place is kept

Was: a reconnect or reload could jump you away from what you were reading and re-stamp every turn to now. →

Now: you stay where you were, history ends where it should, each turn keeps its own time and session, and opening a conversation lands on the latest turn.

#### Easier to find and start conversations

Was: grouped session rows looked alike, a search result kept its filter, and New session hid why it couldn't start. →

Now: rows lead with the session and what it last did, search clears when you open a result, and New session says plainly what is needed, with a copyable command.

#### Calmer on a phone

Was: the Attention panel could lose its place after the phone slept, and the footer overflowed with long machine names. →

Now: Attention opens as a proper sheet that keeps focus, and the footer names the machine you can't reach.


## Under the hood

### Only reply (dev)

#### Connection state machine

Was: a stale opening could open a second machine socket whose handshake timeout downgraded the page, and blockAuthentication left the heartbeat running so a refused pairing flipped back to live. →

Now: machine sockets carry a generation, a superseded socket only closes itself, and an auth block stops the heartbeat until re-pairing.

#### Persistent conversation store

Was: drafts, pending sends and turn times were memory-only. →

Now: a per-conversation ConversationStore persists them with write and read bounds, purges a machine's entries on revoke or removal, and never auto-sends an over-bound or malformed entry.

#### History anchoring

Was: a reconnect re-offered "Load earlier" and the browser silently reset scroll, and late file-card growth left a following thread short of the tail. →

Now: the history cursor survives reconnects, the reader's turn is restored, and the thread re-pins on content-height changes while following.

#### Delivery proof binds to the parked anchor

Was: close and verification measured a worker's live HEAD, so a worker moving to another task invalidated its parked delivery's proof. →

Now: a delivery reachable from its parked or origin factory ref keeps its proof; rewritten heads are still refused. Delivery also retries a declined wake after the turn instead of marking the message read (#1082, #1080, #1084).

#### Release and journey tooling

Was: fast gate rows crashed after a force-push, and several journeys ran on wall-clock budgets that timed out under host load. →

Now: rows fall back to merge-base (#1075), J3/J5/J8/J9 wait on hub-double and DOM events with budgets sized from measured runtime, and every HUB-J12 test leaves receipts.
