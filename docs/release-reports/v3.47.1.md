## What you can do now

### Commander, steadier

#### Held messages go out

Was: with two tabs open, a message held while a machine reconnected could stay unsent, and a tab could stay on "Reconnecting". →

Now: it is sent once the machine is back, and both tabs show it delivered.

#### Pairing that explains itself

Was: when pairing timed out, nothing visibly changed. →

Now: the error appears in view with the next step, and on a phone "Technical details" opens with one tap and keeps your place.

#### Browsers you can recognize

Was: the installations sheet showed generation numbers, timestamps and signing keys. →

Now: each browser has a name and a relative time, with the technical details tucked behind a disclosure.

#### Plainer screens

Was: permission lists, the Connection log and inbox statuses used internal wording. →

Now: they say what happened in plain words, the phone header keeps the machine name whole, and raw details sit behind "Technical details".

#### Tidier command line

Was: `cas list` showed crashed daemons as "stopped" forever, and `cas integrate` output broke at narrow widths. →

Now: crashed daemons read as dead and their sockets are cleaned up, and `cas integrate` fits any terminal.


## Under the hood

### Delivery and release fixes

#### Held-send recovery

Was: a fresh session Welcome during machine SSE backoff left the header Reconnecting, and a no-write claim reset dropped the held history row. →

Now: a live Welcome restarts the machine retry, held sends wake after machine readiness, and the authoritative held transition is restored.

#### Pre-merge ISA gate

Was: the x86_64 AVX-512 audit ran only inside release.sh after main landed. →

Now: a release-binary-isa gate row builds the locked zigbuild artifact and audits it before pr-body, reusing a PASS keyed on lockfile, code and toolchain.

#### Close and admission

Was: a parked child's content proof walked generated bundles (29s), and admission lock fds leaked into sccache servers and orphaned test children. →

Now: the proof leaves regenerable artifacts to the bundle rule (2s), and a lease-holder process keeps the locks while commands get none.

#### Factory and QA tooling

Was: dead daemons stayed "stopped", cancelled QA tasks dead-ended their rounds, and visual-qa flagged input scroll, line clamps and JS-required pages. →

Now: dead daemons are reaped safely, orphaned rounds are withdrawn and redispatched, and those inspector false positives are gone.

#### CI and receipts

Was: one-worker journeys pushed queue runs past the check timeout, and announce skipped the draft's empty POSTED table. →

Now: CI runs journeys on four workers, and announce writes the message ids into the draft.
