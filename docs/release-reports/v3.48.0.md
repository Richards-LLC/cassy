## What you can do now

### Commander, in Glass and steadier

#### A new look: Glass

Was: flat paper-and-graphite panels. →

Now: a violet-to-teal aurora behind frosted panels, a glowing violet Send and a warm amber-to-coral card for questions, in light and dark, with text kept easy to read, solid panels for forced colours, more contrast or reduced transparency, and no motion.

#### No more flickering "Reconnecting"

Was: a brief hiccup in Commander's update feed dropped every working conversation and the header flickered "Reconnecting". →

Now: conversations that are still talking stay connected and read Live.

#### Calmer recovery

Was: a shaky connection retried at full speed and the conversation list could empty out. →

Now: retries back off and the list stays filled while Commander recovers.

#### "Send again" settles

Was: a message resent with "Send again" could stay on "Waiting for … to confirm" after it arrived. →

Now: it settles as Delivered, with one copy in the conversation.


## Under the hood

### Commander and release fixes

#### Glass look

Was: the paper/graphite token set. →

Now: `glass.css` re-colours the generated roles with an aurora under frosted chrome panels, every gradient and blur inside `forced-colors: none`, opaque panels under more contrast or reduced transparency, no animation, and `glass.test.ts` pinning every text pair at 4.5:1 or better.

#### Attach-storm fix

Was: every event-stream reconnect abandoned all terminal sockets, so each SSE flap re-attached healthy sessions. →

Now: only sockets silent for four heartbeats, or a proven machine outage, are replaced, and sockets still opening keep their own deadlines.

#### Event backoff and health

Was: stream headers reset the retry streak and the header read only machine state. →

Now: the streak resets after 10 s of stable stream, a session with recent frames reads Live, and recovery refreshes the catalog before going live.

#### Resend receipts

Was: retiring a legacy socket closed it before an in-flight ack arrived, so "Send again" stayed unconfirmed. →

Now: a retired socket drains for 5 s, accepting only known client_ref and target receipts under the current credential fence.

#### Integration proves no-build rows

Was: markdown-lint and script-tier failures surfaced only in the cut. →

Now: rolling integration records the 11 fast rows and ci-script-tests against its tip, with every train control exported, and preflight refuses missing, stale or failed rows.

#### Suite environment scrub

Was: suite children inherited train control variables. →

Now: `release-test-env.sh` strips identity and train/gate namespaces, sets a clean HOME and disables global Git config, keeping memory admission and linker context.

#### Lane compile proof

Was: previews nested under a scratch directory were refused by private target ownership before Cargo ran. →

Now: previews are direct worktree children with bound sibling provenance, and GC still handles older nested previews safely.

#### Compiler cache and build leases

Was: a cold sccache client handed the inherited target lease to its long-lived server, so the next capped test failed with EAGAIN. →

Now: capped runs blank both rustc wrappers, while real builders keep the lease and assembly and CI builds keep sccache.
