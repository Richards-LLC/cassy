What you can do now

02 / 06
01

Sending

Held messages go out after reconnect

Wasif the machine came back mid-send, a held message could sit unsent with nothing trying it again. →

Nowit goes out promptly once Commander reconnects, exactly once.

No Retry while another tab is sending

Waswith two tabs open, "Retry" could flash in one tab while the other was still sending, inviting a double send. →

Nowboth tabs wait for that send to confirm, and a new reply no longer cuts the wait short.

Polish

Long machine names read in full on phones

Wasa long machine name was cut off beside its codename. →

Nowthe name gets its own row and reads whole, and the codename steps aside when space runs out.

Stop result stays in view

Wasafter Stop, the result line could scroll out of sight. →

Nowit stays on screen beside the next row, and the confirmation says the task goes back to Open.

Revoking says what happened

Wasrevoking this browser's own access dropped you on a silent first screen. →

NowCommander says the access was revoked and puts you on Pair a machine.

Plain words for browser problems

Wasa blocked permission or an unsupported browser read "Can't reach". →

Nowit reads "Blocked by browser" or "Browser can't connect".

Keyboard reaches Interrupt quickly

WasInterrupt was about a dozen Tab presses from search. →

Now"Skip to the conversation" gets you there in three keys, with a ring at every stop.

Pinned bar readable on hover in dark

Washovering the pinned question bar in dark turned it navy under dark text. →

Nowit keeps its amber fill and only lifts.

Assign names its task

Wasthe Assign picker showed only the task's ID. →

Nowit names the task by its title.

Landscape refusals stay in view

Wason a phone held sideways, a refusal in Tasks & progress landed below the fold. →

Nowit rides the bottom edge of the sheet.

Undo dismiss says what happens

Wasdismissing the phone Undo offer gave no hint where Undo went. →

Nowthe dismiss button says Undo stays in Tasks & progress, and it does.

Lighter pairing buttons

Nowit is part of the sheet and frosts only while fields scroll beneath it.

Answers minutes apart get their own time

Nowa five-minute gap starts a new group with its own time, and reload keeps the grouping.

Clearer during an outage

Wasa reload mid-outage read "0 conversations" and the banner covered the thread's top. →

Nowit reads "Conversations not loaded yet" and the banner sits above the thread.

Who is in control

Nowthe header names that device, and a refusal says how to get past it.

Plainer pairing

Nowit reads "See and revoke other browsers on" the machine, and a browser is named like "Chrome on Linux".

Under the hood

03 / 06
01

Commander send fixes, polish and journey tooling

Held-send wake coalescing

WasHeldSendFlushes returned early while a batch held the key, so a Live callback during a definite no-write settlement was lost and the message stalled. →

Nowa mid-batch call marks the batch to rerun with the latest work, the journal still gates every write, and uncertain or revoked claims never replay.

Peer receipt deadlines

Wasa tab projecting another tab's durable sending claim marked it unconfirmed at once and showed Retry. →

Nowit keeps the claim's original 15 s receipt clock, and the journal refuses to re-hold an active peer claim even from a stale Retry.

Peer clocks ignore local replies

Wasa reply landing in one tab applied the 5 s reply grace to a peer's send. →

Nowrestored projections keep the original timeout, a lost dispatch claim reads durable state instead of unconfirming, and history settles restored sends as one row.

Phone header and Stop result

Wasthe phone identity shared a row with the actions, so names truncated beside the codename. →

Nowidentity has its own row, the machine never shrinks while the codename shows, and Stop scrolls its result into view.

Browser-side status

Wasblocked permission and unsupported browser fell through to "Can't reach", and one session's retry set the machine footer to Reconnecting. →

NowBROWSER_BLOCKED and BROWSER_UNSUPPORTED labels reach header, row, footer and launch sheet, and the footer ignores a sibling retry.

Keyboard, control and pairing copy

WasInterrupt was 12 Tabs from search, control holders were hidden, and pairing showed raw scope ids. →

Nowa skip link, a header control line, plain admin consent, a browserLabel default name and aria-labelledby on every pair dialog.

Thread time groups and kept replies

Wasturns minutes apart merged under one time, and a journal-restored reply's stored time posed as a machine stamp. →

NowGROUP_SPLIT_MS splits on a five-minute gap, and kept replies take the machine stamp once history arrives.

Glass details

Wasthe pinned bar lost its amber on hover in dark, the rail sliced rows at its corners, and the pair action bar frosted into a slab. →

Nowhover keeps the ask fill, rail edges fade only with rows beyond, and the bar frosts only over scrolled fields.

Fleet sheet details

Wasthe Assign picker showed only an id, a landscape refusal fell below the fold, and dismissing Undo was ambiguous. →

Nowthe picker names the task title, the notice is sticky at the sheet's bottom, and the dismiss label says Undo stays.

Journey selection at the reviewed head

Wasthe close gate and journey-receipt.py ran journeys-for-diff.py from the shared checkout, whose older copy could ignore CAS_JOURNEYS_HEAD. →

Nowboth run the selector blob from the reviewed head over stdin and refuse proof if it is missing or broken.
