## What you can do now

### Safer next steps

#### Wait for results

Was: a change could be combined before its checks finished.

Now: it waits for them, and a later failure raises an urgent alert naming the affected branch.

#### Check related parts

Was: a change could miss checks in parts that use it.

Now: those parts need their own passing checks before the change is handed over.

#### Catch release failures sooner

Was: a release could enter the final queue before its first checks finished.

Now: it waits, and a failure names the broken checks and tests.

#### Questions with a next step

Was: an unanswered question could leave work waiting indefinitely.

Now: it can include a deadline and a default, with a reminder to proceed when time runs out.

#### Unstick ready work

Was: ready work could sit waiting without an urgent alert.

Now: it raises one when capacity sits idle, while changes still being checked stay out of the ready list.


## Under the hood

### Validation and compatibility

#### Merge admission

Was: pending lane CI was advisory for code.

Now: `worktree_merge` refuses it without override, and a red merged commit raises a critical blocker naming its integration branch.

#### Dependent coverage

Was: blast-radius proof covered changed modules.

Now: direct workspace dependents also need passing targeted-test receipts at the delivered commit.

#### Release admission

Was: `release.sh` enabled auto-merge immediately.

Now: it waits for Scoped Validation; red or cancelled checks stop with exit 5 and failing check/test names.

#### Commander deadlines

Was: asks had no deadline or default.

Now: `kind=ask` accepts `remind_delay_secs` with `remind_message`, schedules a proceed reminder and returns its cancellation ID.

#### Ready-work alerts

Was: stalled ready work emitted a routine notice and pending deliveries counted as ready.

Now: the notice raises a blocker and excludes deliveries still awaiting CI.

#### OpenCode configuration

Was: the projection test pinned full prompt text.

Now: it checks both agents' modes, models, variants, permissions, non-empty prompts and top-level keys.

#### Latency checks

Was: tight upper bounds failed on busy runners.

Now: injected-latency lower bounds and success assertions remain, with 10-times-RTT upper hang guards.
