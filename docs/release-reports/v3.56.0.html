What you can do now

02 / 06
01

Factory

No freezes while waiting

Wasthe screen could stall while checking on outside events.

Nowthose checks run in the background, so typing and rendering stay responsive.

Messages arrive

Wasa message to a teammate could sit undelivered while later messages got through.

Noweach message is delivered and marked read only after the recipient actually receives it.

Fewer false alarms

Waspeople could be told a conversation was abandoned or a busy process had stalled when neither was true.

Nowthose warnings are tied to observed activity and real outcomes.

Your phone sees the conversation

Wassome replies never reached Commander, and the phone could show a question without its answer.

Nowreplies are mirrored, and the phone shows terminal questions together with their replies. Operators can turn the automatic reply mirror off and on.

Starting a session

Wasstartup could time out while waiting for the CAS server, and a build mismatch could fail late.

Nowstartup honors the wait window, and a known daemon/provisioner mismatch is caught before launch with a recovery command.

Stop and reset commands

Wasa stop or context reset could fail silently under store contention.

Nowthe action takes effect or leaves a recorded failure with a reason.

Under the hood

03 / 06
01

Factory

Reminder probes

Wasexternal branch_contained_in, tag_exists, and ci_completed probes ran git fetch and gh on the daemon loop.

Nowprobes run off-loop, fetch each ref once per pass, and back off or expire stale reminders.

Teams inbox

Wasa Claude Teams member's messages stayed gated.

Nowthe <name>@<session> member owns its inbox, messages surface at tool boundaries and turn start, and confirmation needs a transport receipt.

Spawn validation

Wasboot checks could kill Claude launches waiting on the CAS server, and build drift could fail only during provision.

Nowthe startup wait is honored, a positive mismatch is refused before queueing, and inconclusive identity checks say they were skipped so provision-time validation remains the backstop.

Delivery receipts and merge gates

WasQA waivers could follow the wrong branch, a receipt could include a later merge's files, and a merge request could be suppressed as already landed when it was not.

Nowwaivers and proof bind to the delivery branch and the receipt's first-parent diff; unresolved deliveries are refused before close, and a park sends one notification.

Worker lifecycle

Wasshutdown_workers and clear_context could silently fail when the daemon's store wait budget was spent.

Nowshutdown works under contention, while a failed recycle records a terminal result and tells the supervisor why.

Coordination and tools

Wasone missing remind requirement hid the rest, verification rejected issues arrays, and broadcasts could not be replaced or delivered to later joiners.

Nowrefusals list every missing requirement, verification accepts JSON arrays, and broadcasts support replacement keys and later-joiner delivery. Tool schemas stay within the 3,600-byte cap.

Artifact uploads

Wasartifact publishing rejected WebM QA recordings even when they were below the size limit.

Nowvideo/webm recordings can be published as durable task artifacts.

Test and documentation maintenance

Washub-web lifecycle tests could race under load, and test-shape pins rejected valid rebased lanes.

Nowthe lifecycle tests are stable under concurrency, valid pins survive rebases, and the communication contract, codemap, and product overview are current.
