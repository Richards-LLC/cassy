## What you can do now

### Factory

#### No freezes while waiting

Was: the screen could stall while checking on outside events.

Now: those checks run in the background, so typing and rendering stay responsive.

#### Messages arrive

Was: a message to a teammate could sit undelivered while later messages got through.

Now: each message is delivered and marked read only after the recipient actually receives it.

#### Fewer false alarms

Was: people could be told a conversation was abandoned or a busy process had stalled when neither was true.

Now: those warnings are tied to observed activity and real outcomes.

#### Your phone sees the conversation

Was: some replies never reached Commander, and the phone could show a question without its answer.

Now: replies are mirrored, and the phone shows terminal questions together with their replies. Operators can turn the automatic reply mirror off and on.

#### Starting a session

Was: startup could time out while waiting for the CAS server, and a build mismatch could fail late.

Now: startup honors the wait window, and a known daemon/provisioner mismatch is caught before launch with a recovery command.

#### Stop and reset commands

Was: a stop or context reset could fail silently under store contention.

Now: the action takes effect or leaves a recorded failure with a reason.

## Under the hood

### Factory

#### Reminder probes

Was: external `branch_contained_in`, `tag_exists`, and `ci_completed` probes ran `git fetch` and `gh` on the daemon loop.

Now: probes run off-loop, fetch each ref once per pass, and back off or expire stale reminders.

#### Teams inbox

Was: a Claude Teams member's messages stayed gated.

Now: the `<name>@<session>` member owns its inbox, messages surface at tool boundaries and turn start, and confirmation needs a transport receipt.

#### Spawn validation

Was: boot checks could kill Claude launches waiting on the CAS server, and build drift could fail only during provision.

Now: the startup wait is honored, a positive mismatch is refused before queueing, and inconclusive identity checks say they were skipped so provision-time validation remains the backstop.

#### Delivery receipts and merge gates

Was: QA waivers could follow the wrong branch, a receipt could include a later merge's files, and a merge request could be suppressed as already landed when it was not.

Now: waivers and proof bind to the delivery branch and the receipt's first-parent diff; unresolved deliveries are refused before close, and a park sends one notification.

#### Worker lifecycle

Was: `shutdown_workers` and `clear_context` could silently fail when the daemon's store wait budget was spent.

Now: shutdown works under contention, while a failed recycle records a terminal result and tells the supervisor why.

#### Coordination and tools

Was: one missing `remind` requirement hid the rest, verification rejected `issues` arrays, and broadcasts could not be replaced or delivered to later joiners.

Now: refusals list every missing requirement, verification accepts JSON arrays, and broadcasts support replacement keys and later-joiner delivery. Tool schemas stay within the 3,600-byte cap.

#### Artifact uploads

Was: artifact publishing rejected WebM QA recordings even when they were below the size limit.

Now: `video/webm` recordings can be published as durable task artifacts.

#### Test and documentation maintenance

Was: hub-web lifecycle tests could race under load, and test-shape pins rejected valid rebased lanes.

Now: the lifecycle tests are stable under concurrency, valid pins survive rebases, and the communication contract, codemap, and product overview are current.
