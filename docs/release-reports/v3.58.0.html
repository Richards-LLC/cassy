What you can do now

02 / 06
01

Messages arrive

Messages mid-task

Wasinstructions to a busy helper waited 15 minutes, then were dropped.

Nowthe helper sees them at its next step.

Nothing lost in the inbox

Wasa message checked mid-task but never handled could vanish.

Nowit comes back once until it is handled.

Clear send outcome

Wassending to a busy helper gave no outcome.

Nowyou see whether it was delivered or when it will arrive.

Clear waiting status

Wasa waiting message's status was ambiguous.

Nowthe status says why it is waiting.

Replies stay in the thread

Wassome replies were rejected as not part of the conversation.

Nowreplies and follow-ups thread as expected.

Reminders get noticed

Wasa reminder could sit unseen until something else happened.

Nowa reminder wakes an idle lead session right away.

Mail survives restarts

Wasmessages sent while a lead session restarted could be lost.

Nowthey arrive once it is back.

Fewer notices

Wasa flood of "message abandoned" notices, and Commander sends queued for 15 minutes.

Nowone hourly summary, and an immediate "no Commander attached" reply.

Handoffs

Waswork from a session that ended sat unowned, and conflicts went unnoticed.

Nowthe work is flagged for handoff and both sides are told.

02

Fewer false alarms

Busy is not stuck

Wasbusy helpers were reported as gone quiet.

Nowonly truly idle helpers trigger the warning.

No merge nags

Wasrepeated "merge now" nags for work already merged or still in checks.

Nowone quiet note, and silence while checks run.

Honest merge requests

Wasevery merge request said its proof was missing.

Nowit shows the proof that was sent.

Clean takeovers

Wasa replacement was told to wait on a crashed session's claim.

Nowthe takeover is clean, with no false conflict.

Closing finished work

Wasshipped work could block closing a larger piece of work.

Nowit no longer does.

Clearer test-proof checks

Wasvalid test results were refused without a reason.

Nowthey are accepted, and a refusal says what is missing.

Stated message limits

Wasmessage size limits were hidden until a message bounced.

Nowlimits and reminder rules are stated up front.

03

Smoother screens and restarts

Smooth typing

Wasthe screen could freeze for seconds while background checks ran or messages were sent.

Nowtyping stays responsive.

Restarts keep their place

Wasbringing a session back by name failed silently, and Claude sessions could restart on the wrong tool.

Nowthey resume in their workspace on Claude, or show why not.

Finished work keeps its checks

Wasstarting the next task could cancel checks on finished work.

Nowfinished work keeps its checks and merges as reviewed.

04

Slack, Commander and the terminal

Slack after updates

Wasafter an update, Slack reading and posting could fail with a missing sign-in.

Nowcas update repairs the setting, and saved sign-ins are used.

Commander permission notes

Wasa note said to "add it" in a screen with no way to add it.

Nowone short note points to pairing again.

Reading earlier turns

Wasin Commander, scrolling up to read earlier turns could jump back to the newest message.

Nowthe view stays where you are reading.

Apps menu

Wasthe Cassy Cloud apps menu stayed open over Commander after opening Explorer.

Nowit closes, so Commander is as you left it.

Readable terminal text

Waserror lines and settings lists were faint on light terminals.

Nowthey are readable, and plain terminals get plain text.

Terminal checks

Wasterminal checks silently did nothing from a linked folder.

Nowthey run and print a receipt.

Under the hood

03 / 06
01

Message delivery

Held deliveries (#1223)

Wasgated_not_ready rows were hidden from PostToolUse and abandoned after 900 s.

Nowthe gate holds only the PTY nudge; held rows surface at the next tool boundary.

Held-state status

Wasdeferred_inbox_at was never cleared, and held-wake and composer retry looked the same.

Nowcleared on a new inject; typed held state; exhausted stamps warn.

Poll resurface (#1215 e)

Wasinbox_poll was an at-most-once claim.

Nowunacked rows resurface once per turn; m273 records poll_settled.

Named send outcome

Wassends returned "enqueued… check message_status".

Nowa ≤1 s wait returns delivered, deferred with the reason, or no decision yet.

Reply threading

Wasin_reply_to required the prior row's source to equal the literal target.

Nowparty-normalised matching; broadcasts and lifecycle rows are valid references.

Reminder wakes

Wasfired reminders had no origin stamp and never woke a pane.

Nowdaemon-stamped SupervisorWakeClass::Reminder, sourced from the owner.

Restart-safe mail

Wasmail to a relaunched supervisor's old agent id was never claimed.

Nowsame-lineage claims deliver exactly once; m275 adds an (event_type, created_at) index.

Abandonment episodes (#1196)

Wasone notice per abandoned row, and operator sends queued 900 s.

Nowhourly episodes; immediate refusal when commander_clients=0.

Epic handoff (#1186)

WasSessionEnd left owned epics unowned, and owner notices had no liveness check.

Noworphan notices, live-owner routing with operator fallback, dual conflict notices.

02

Stall detection and merges

Activity signals

Wasthe stall check trusted a cold transcript and ignored Codex custom calls and MCP calls.

Nowturn, CPU, HEAD age, custom calls and MCP calls count; MCP activity is throttled to one event per 30 s.

Stall containment (#1225)

Wassupervisor_stalled keyed on awaiting_merge and ignored pending CI reminders.

Nowcontainment is cached per tip, notices are deduped, and pending ci_completed/branch_contained_in reminders hold the alarm.

Merge-request evidence

Wasthe review block always said "Evidence not supplied".

Nowevidence is parsed from proof:/ci: lines and the QA bundle.

Parked tips

Wasthe next push cancelled a parked SHA's CI, and worktree_merge took the moving tip.

Nowthe tip is kept on factory/<worker>-<task>; merges go by anchor.

Cloud claim release

Wasa reset freed only the local lease, so PEER CLAIM LOST fired at the new assignee.

Nowdead holders' Cloud claims are settled or treated as lapsed.

Stranded-branch gate

Wasreused lanes counted other tasks' commits as stranded.

Nowforeign-only lane commits count as zero.

Loaded-proof gate (#1222)

Wastarget=explicit non-Rust failed has_target_scope with no detail.

Nowa structured target= whitelist with per-note parsed/missing diagnostics.

Coordination schema

Wasthe schema omitted body caps and cross_session and stated a 3600 s external TTL.

Nowdocumented limits, a 604800 s TTL, and shared alias parsing.

03

Factory loop (#1211)

Queue jobs

Wasprompt-queue selection, stamps and settles ran inline on the loop thread.

NowQueueSelect/QueueStamp/QueueSettle jobs; failed stamps are deferred, never dropped.

Director and probes

Wassnapshot, health-probe, reminder and stall-file I/O ran on the loop.

Nowread, write and blocking lane jobs; RevalidateFresh never uses the cache.

Non-blocking inject

Wasinjects awaited 1.2–5.2 s inline and PTY writes blocked.

Nowa per-pane inject state machine, buffered non-blocking writes, and a two-phase ClaimForInject.

04

Lifecycle, CLI and tooling

Same-name respawn

Wasprovisioning refused any branch but factory/<w>, and failures stayed UNCONFIRMED.

Nowthe clean own-task lane is reused; failures are terminal FAILED.

Safe recycle

WasClaude workers inherited the supervisor's CLI, and recycle stopped them before validating.

NowCAS_FACTORY_WORKER_CLI=claude; the recipe is validated before stop.

Violet credentials

Wascas violet needed an env token, and project proxy.toml kept a stale env ref.

Nowa shared resolver with file fallback; cas update rewrites stale refs.

Readable CLI colour

Waserror lines at 3.08:1 contrast, colour under NO_COLOR, and ✗ under LC_ALL=C.

Nowmark-only colour, NO_COLOR honoured, [ERROR] fallback, and config text at ≥4.5:1.

Terminal QA symlinks

Wascomparing argv[1] with import.meta.url skipped main() through a symlink.

Nowthe guard compares realpaths.

Commander copy and app switcher

Waspermission copy was duplicated, and the switcher popover stayed open after Explorer.

Nowconsolidated rail copy; hidePopover on activation with focus kept.

Transcript scroll pin

Wasthe 5 s heartbeat render could pin the transcript to the tail before the reader's scroll event landed, so "Load earlier turns" snapped back.

NowpinUnlessUnseen defers the pin by one animation frame and re-checks the reader's position.

Release latency receipt

Wasthe receipt required a tag-push release.yml run and exited 1.

Nowmain-push and gate-green starts; the report adds "Main to published".

Honest test proofs

Wasnamed-test runs inherited the host PATH, and two tests read the live /proc.

NowPATH is limited to toolchain plus /usr/bin:/bin; probes are injected.

Multi-supervisor spec

Wasthere was no protocol for cross-supervisor claims or staging merges.

Nowa design spec proposes claims, a merge lock and an ownership view; not yet built.
