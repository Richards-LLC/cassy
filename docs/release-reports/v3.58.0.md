Published 16 October 2026 · 1:07 AM ET

## What you can do now

### Messages arrive

#### Messages mid-task

Was: instructions to a busy helper waited 15 minutes, then were dropped.

Now: the helper sees them at its next step.

#### Nothing lost in the inbox

Was: a message checked mid-task but never handled could vanish.

Now: it comes back once until it is handled.

#### Clear send outcome

Was: sending to a busy helper gave no outcome.

Now: you see whether it was delivered or when it will arrive.

#### Clear waiting status

Was: a waiting message's status was ambiguous.

Now: the status says why it is waiting.

#### Replies stay in the thread

Was: some replies were rejected as not part of the conversation.

Now: replies and follow-ups thread as expected.

#### Reminders get noticed

Was: a reminder could sit unseen until something else happened.

Now: a reminder wakes an idle lead session right away.

#### Mail survives restarts

Was: messages sent while a lead session restarted could be lost.

Now: they arrive once it is back.

#### Fewer notices

Was: a flood of "message abandoned" notices, and Commander sends queued for 15 minutes.

Now: one hourly summary, and an immediate "no Commander attached" reply.

#### Handoffs

Was: work from a session that ended sat unowned, and conflicts went unnoticed.

Now: the work is flagged for handoff and both sides are told.

### Fewer false alarms

#### Busy is not stuck

Was: busy helpers were reported as gone quiet.

Now: only truly idle helpers trigger the warning.

#### No merge nags

Was: repeated "merge now" nags for work already merged or still in checks.

Now: one quiet note, and silence while checks run.

#### Honest merge requests

Was: every merge request said its proof was missing.

Now: it shows the proof that was sent.

#### Clean takeovers

Was: a replacement was told to wait on a crashed session's claim.

Now: the takeover is clean, with no false conflict.

#### Closing finished work

Was: shipped work could block closing a larger piece of work.

Now: it no longer does.

#### Clearer test-proof checks

Was: valid test results were refused without a reason.

Now: they are accepted, and a refusal says what is missing.

#### Stated message limits

Was: message size limits were hidden until a message bounced.

Now: limits and reminder rules are stated up front.

### Smoother screens and restarts

#### Smooth typing

Was: the screen could freeze for seconds while background checks ran or messages were sent.

Now: typing stays responsive.

#### Restarts keep their place

Was: bringing a session back by name failed silently, and Claude sessions could restart on the wrong tool.

Now: they resume in their workspace on Claude, or show why not.

#### Finished work keeps its checks

Was: starting the next task could cancel checks on finished work.

Now: finished work keeps its checks and merges as reviewed.

### Slack, Commander and the terminal

#### Slack after updates

Was: after an update, Slack reading and posting could fail with a missing sign-in.

Now: `cas update` repairs the setting, and saved sign-ins are used.

#### Commander permission notes

Was: a note said to "add it" in a screen with no way to add it.

Now: one short note points to pairing again.

#### Reading earlier turns

Was: in Commander, scrolling up to read earlier turns could jump back to the newest message.

Now: the view stays where you are reading.

#### Apps menu

Was: the Cassy Cloud apps menu stayed open over Commander after opening Explorer.

Now: it closes, so Commander is as you left it.

#### Readable terminal text

Was: error lines and settings lists were faint on light terminals.

Now: they are readable, and plain terminals get plain text.

#### Terminal checks

Was: terminal checks silently did nothing from a linked folder.

Now: they run and print a receipt.


## Under the hood

### Message delivery

#### Held deliveries (#1223)

Was: `gated_not_ready` rows were hidden from PostToolUse and abandoned after 900 s.

Now: the gate holds only the PTY nudge; held rows surface at the next tool boundary.

#### Held-state status

Was: `deferred_inbox_at` was never cleared, and held-wake and composer retry looked the same.

Now: cleared on a new inject; typed held state; exhausted stamps warn.

#### Poll resurface (#1215 e)

Was: `inbox_poll` was an at-most-once claim.

Now: unacked rows resurface once per turn; m273 records `poll_settled`.

#### Named send outcome

Was: sends returned "enqueued… check message_status".

Now: a ≤1 s wait returns delivered, deferred with the reason, or no decision yet.

#### Reply threading

Was: `in_reply_to` required the prior row's source to equal the literal target.

Now: party-normalised matching; broadcasts and lifecycle rows are valid references.

#### Reminder wakes

Was: fired reminders had no origin stamp and never woke a pane.

Now: daemon-stamped `SupervisorWakeClass::Reminder`, sourced from the owner.

#### Restart-safe mail

Was: mail to a relaunched supervisor's old agent id was never claimed.

Now: same-lineage claims deliver exactly once; m275 adds an `(event_type, created_at)` index.

#### Abandonment episodes (#1196)

Was: one notice per abandoned row, and operator sends queued 900 s.

Now: hourly episodes; immediate refusal when `commander_clients=0`.

#### Epic handoff (#1186)

Was: SessionEnd left owned epics unowned, and owner notices had no liveness check.

Now: orphan notices, live-owner routing with operator fallback, dual conflict notices.

### Stall detection and merges

#### Activity signals

Was: the stall check trusted a cold transcript and ignored Codex custom calls and MCP calls.

Now: turn, CPU, HEAD age, custom calls and MCP calls count; MCP activity is throttled to one event per 30 s.

#### Stall containment (#1225)

Was: `supervisor_stalled` keyed on `awaiting_merge` and ignored pending CI reminders.

Now: containment is cached per tip, notices are deduped, and pending `ci_completed`/`branch_contained_in` reminders hold the alarm.

#### Merge-request evidence

Was: the review block always said "Evidence not supplied".

Now: evidence is parsed from `proof:`/`ci:` lines and the QA bundle.

#### Parked tips

Was: the next push cancelled a parked SHA's CI, and `worktree_merge` took the moving tip.

Now: the tip is kept on `factory/<worker>-<task>`; merges go by anchor.

#### Cloud claim release

Was: a reset freed only the local lease, so PEER CLAIM LOST fired at the new assignee.

Now: dead holders' Cloud claims are settled or treated as lapsed.

#### Stranded-branch gate

Was: reused lanes counted other tasks' commits as stranded.

Now: foreign-only lane commits count as zero.

#### Loaded-proof gate (#1222)

Was: `target=explicit non-Rust` failed `has_target_scope` with no detail.

Now: a structured `target=` whitelist with per-note parsed/missing diagnostics.

#### Coordination schema

Was: the schema omitted body caps and `cross_session` and stated a 3600 s external TTL.

Now: documented limits, a 604800 s TTL, and shared alias parsing.

### Factory loop (#1211)

#### Queue jobs

Was: prompt-queue selection, stamps and settles ran inline on the loop thread.

Now: QueueSelect/QueueStamp/QueueSettle jobs; failed stamps are deferred, never dropped.

#### Director and probes

Was: snapshot, health-probe, reminder and stall-file I/O ran on the loop.

Now: read, write and blocking lane jobs; `RevalidateFresh` never uses the cache.

#### Non-blocking inject

Was: injects awaited 1.2–5.2 s inline and PTY writes blocked.

Now: a per-pane inject state machine, buffered non-blocking writes, and a two-phase `ClaimForInject`.

### Lifecycle, CLI and tooling

#### Same-name respawn

Was: provisioning refused any branch but `factory/<w>`, and failures stayed UNCONFIRMED.

Now: the clean own-task lane is reused; failures are terminal FAILED.

#### Safe recycle

Was: Claude workers inherited the supervisor's CLI, and recycle stopped them before validating.

Now: `CAS_FACTORY_WORKER_CLI=claude`; the recipe is validated before stop.

#### Violet credentials

Was: `cas violet` needed an env token, and project `proxy.toml` kept a stale env ref.

Now: a shared resolver with file fallback; `cas update` rewrites stale refs.

#### Readable CLI colour

Was: error lines at 3.08:1 contrast, colour under `NO_COLOR`, and ✗ under `LC_ALL=C`.

Now: mark-only colour, `NO_COLOR` honoured, `[ERROR]` fallback, and config text at ≥4.5:1.

#### Terminal QA symlinks

Was: comparing `argv[1]` with `import.meta.url` skipped `main()` through a symlink.

Now: the guard compares realpaths.

#### Commander copy and app switcher

Was: permission copy was duplicated, and the switcher popover stayed open after Explorer.

Now: consolidated rail copy; `hidePopover` on activation with focus kept.

#### Transcript scroll pin

Was: the 5 s heartbeat render could pin the transcript to the tail before the reader's scroll event landed, so "Load earlier turns" snapped back.

Now: `pinUnlessUnseen` defers the pin by one animation frame and re-checks the reader's position.

#### Release latency receipt

Was: the receipt required a tag-push `release.yml` run and exited 1.

Now: main-push and gate-green starts; the report adds "Main to published".

#### Honest test proofs

Was: named-test runs inherited the host PATH, and two tests read the live /proc.

Now: PATH is limited to toolchain plus `/usr/bin:/bin`; probes are injected.

#### Multi-supervisor spec

Was: there was no protocol for cross-supervisor claims or staging merges.

Now: a design spec proposes claims, a merge lock and an ownership view; not yet built.
