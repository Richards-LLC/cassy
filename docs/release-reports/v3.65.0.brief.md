# Brief: Cassy v3.65.0 release report

Prepared with cas-release-report, cas-html-reports and cas-ui-craft.

## Single idea

Thirty-eight changes span five areas, led by releases and sessions (20 of the 38 entries).

## Hero form

A sandstone serif verdict above a directly labelled equal-dot change map. One dot represents one of the 38 bold-titled CHANGELOG entries, rather than one of its seven section headings. The largest group is Releases (11), followed by Sessions (9), Commander (7), Slack and Cloud (6) and Messages (5). Preserve the established area order, not a rank order. The complete verdict and figure must fit at 1280×800 and 390×844.

## Emotional register

Assured, humane and collected: warm sandstone paper, a regular serif argument, system sans reading text, mono folio and quiet rules. No root DESIGN.md exists; the fixed release-report renderer uses the project's docs/design/design-tokens.json roles and retains the Petrastella release identity. cas-html-reports supplies the source/HTML pairing; cas-ui-craft supplies the visual critique.

## Distinctive move

Thirty-eight equal-area dots show the distribution of shipped changes, without equating change count to effort or impact. Each dot has its change title, with the same data in the adjacent open table. The eleven-dot Releases row earns the accent; together with nine Sessions changes it forms a majority of the named changes. The quiet decorative row returns at Install.

## Deliberately omitted

No invented speedup or issue count. The generator's referenced-issue window ended at 12:51:20Z; subsequent reconciliation is listed separately, without altering its zero counted issues. The report does not claim the wider consumer-Mac hub flow or Gatekeeper/SIP GUI checklist ran. It names the known-root post-update refresh stall as a separate follow-up. No zero or unmeasured statistic appears on page one.
