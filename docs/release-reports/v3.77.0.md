## What you can do now

### Starting and delivering work

#### Adding help from an out-of-date copy

Was: Asking for another assistant while your copy had drifted from the shared one said "queued", then failed out of sight.

Now: It is refused at once with the command that fixes the copy, and any later start failure is reported straight away.

#### A second task while the first waits

Was: Starting a new task while earlier work waited to merge could move that work's branch and suggest a push that would overwrite it.

Now: The new task gets its own branch, the waiting work stays put, and its backup copy can be saved.

#### Moving work onto a corrected base

Was: After work moved onto a corrected starting point, Cassy said the branch held nothing to deliver, forcing a redo.

Now: It recognizes the task's own commits and records where the work used to be, while empty or unrelated branches are still refused.

#### Closing again after bringing in the latest changes

Was: Closing again after merging in the latest shared changes reviewed the old version, then blamed history that was fine.

Now: The new version is the one reviewed and merged, and work that is already merged keeps its original record.

#### Closing merged work after branch cleanup

Was: Once merged work's branch was deleted, closing it was refused until someone pushed the branch again.

Now: It closes from the merged copy, while work that never merged is still refused.

#### Restarting on a recovery branch

Was: Restarting a task on its own recovery branch could be refused as if it belonged to a different task.

Now: It is recognized as the task's own.

### Reviews and reminders

#### Abandoned reviews

Was: When a reviewer let go of a review or shut down, the review stayed locked to them until its deadline.

Now: It is freed at once for someone else, a reviewer can free only their own review, and one who is still active keeps it.

#### Reused review proof

Was: An edit raising how much evidence a check needs could still let older proof count.

Now: Older proof is reused only when every requirement is unchanged, and Cassy names what changed.

#### Timed reminders during background work

Was: A timed reminder could sit unseen for up to two minutes while a background job kept the screen busy.

Now: It is delivered as soon as the current turn has clearly ended, and never typed into a turn still running.

### Build and test reliability

#### Mac test reliability

Was: One of Cassy's own checks failed on every Mac run, and another failed now and then on a busy machine.

Now: Both pass reliably, and the first again covers the real start-up path.


## Under the hood

### Spawn and start

#### Spawn base preflight

Was: spawn_workers answered "Queued … NOT YET CONFIRMED" for a base diverged from origin, and the provision failure arrived as a Normal row at the next turn.

Now: A diverged base is refused synchronously with the rebase --autostash remedy, and provision/register failures are Critical blocker notices that wake an idle supervisor.

#### Second task beside a parked delivery

Was: task start reset factory/<worker> under a parked delivery, close pointed at that branch's push, and the push guard refused the per-task archive ref.

Now: Start leaves the parked branch alone, close measures the task branch with start-base checks, and the hook admits a same-name archive ref but not HEAD:<other-task>.

#### Recovery-suffix branches

Was: factory/<w>-<task>-reviewed parsed as owned by a task named <task>-reviewed, so a restart after the rebind record cleared was refused.

Now: An owner equal to the task ID or prefixed by "<task-id>-" is the task's own, and foreign per-task branches still refuse.

### Delivery recovery

#### rebind_branch after rewritten history

Was: rebind_branch refused rewritten anchors, resets onto origin/<target> and distinct per-task branches with "replacement has no unmerged task delivery".

Now: It attributes commits via the validated common root, derives the base from the target even on the same branch, audits the old start/base/anchor/tip, and still refuses empty or foreign ranges.

#### Re-park at a target-sync tip

Was: close with commit_receipt=C kept the obsolete anchor B and dispatched QA for B, and a later close claimed the start base was not an ancestor of C.

Now: An attributable fast-forward target-sync tip advances the anchor and QA round, a target-reachable tip keeps B even if the content probe is Unknown, and rewrites that drop B refuse truthfully.

#### Merged close after source cleanup

Was: close of a merged AwaitingMerge task refused PARK REFUSED "pushed source at None" once the worker branch was deleted from origin.

Now: A target-merged anchor keeps the content/verification decision instead of re-parking, and an unmerged anchor still refuses.

### QA

#### QA pass claims

Was: Releasing the QA task or shutting down the reviewer left the pass claimed until its deadline, blocking replacement starts and qa_request.

Now: Release returns the pass to Pending (workers drop only their own claim), a claim error warns while Cloud/file-lease cleanup completes, and shutdown/unregister clear stale claims.

#### QA trace rebind coverage

Was: The non-product rebind check compared journey status and invariants only, so a registry edit raising evidence_required reused an old trace.

Now: It also compares kind, evidence_required and the registry safety policy, and the refusal names the journeys and whether the policy changed.

### Wake delivery

#### Self-reminders during background tasks

Was: A Claude supervisor's fired self-reminder needed 2 s of PTY silence, so background-task repaints parked it for 120 s until an unrelated turn.

Now: A turn completion recorded after the last turn start lets it inject once without the silence bar, and the composer, readiness and in-flight vetoes stay.

### Tests

#### macOS provisioning and single-turn flake

Was: provisioning_dispatch_test compared a raw /var tempdir with /private/var and bypassed launch(), and the supervisor single-turn test slept a fixed 200 ms and 5.1 s.

Now: Both sides are canonicalized through a hidden typed-launch hook with the build-hash check, and the test awaits PTY readiness and the consumed submit.
