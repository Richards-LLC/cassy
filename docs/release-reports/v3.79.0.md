## What you can do now

### Release changes

#### Stuck sessions on Mac

Was: A session could go silent while stopping its helpers, with nothing in its log.

Now: Each helper gets 15 seconds to stop; a stall is logged and a session still stuck 20 seconds after a stop request exits.

#### `cas kill` clears dead sessions

Was: `cas kill` could fail to end a stuck session or leave its helper processes running.

Now: It force-stops a stuck session only when sure it is the right process, then cleans up its helpers.

#### Reused process IDs

Was: A session whose process ID now belonged to another program could not be cleared.

Now: `cas kill <name> --force-stale` shows what that ID runs, asks first, and clears the session without touching that program.

#### Taking over from an unresponsive session

Was: After another session took over the work, messages and reset or stop requests still went to the unresponsive one.

Now: They reach the session that took over, which can reset or stop the stranded helpers.

#### Closing tasks

Was: A close running alongside another, or with a note added mid-close, could fail with an unclear message.

Now: Cassy names the close already in progress, keeps notes and retries a note-only race; proof checks also run faster.

#### Shared terminal views

Was: Some views missed a layout refresh when a compact screen held input.

Now: The layout refreshes whenever input moves to or from a compact screen, or a full view resizes.


## Under the hood

### Daemon shutdown and cas kill (GH #1363)

#### Bounded worker teardown

Was: A shutdown's per-worker kill and pane drop ran on the daemon loop and could wedge it silently.

Now: Each runs off-loop with a 15 s deadline that logs the stalled stage and process group; a persisting wedge re-reports every 30 s and exits 20 s after shutdown.

#### Daemon exit and logging

Was: An exited daemon could wait forever on a stuck teardown thread, and the daemon log missed lifecycle events.

Now: Runtime shutdown waits at most 5 s; the daemon log records start, stop and exit.

#### cas kill escalation and reaping

Was: SIGTERM could be ignored, SIGKILL could orphan worker groups, and an unreadable start time read as dead.

Now: SIGKILL needs an exact start-time match; fingerprint-checked worker groups are reaped from the daemon's recorded store only on certain death.

#### --force-stale

Was: A session whose recorded PID ran another program could not be cleared.

Now: `cas kill <name> --force-stale` prints the PID's command, confirms, pins PID and start time, never signals it, reaps workers and drops the record.

### Supervisor adoption (GH #1362)

#### Stranded workers

Was: After epic adoption, target=supervisor routed to the dead supervisor, and reset and shutdown refused from the adopter.

Now: With a live epic_verification_owner and a fingerprint-confirmed dead daemon, routing follows the adopter and reset and shutdown work there.

#### Adopted worker shutdown

Was: A retired adopted orphan blocked later shutdown_workers, and escaped child processes survived.

Now: Retirement is idempotent and shutdown checks the recorded group plus each captured descendant fingerprint.

### Task close

#### Active dispatch conflict

Was: A racing close failed with a generic "different verification proof boundary" error.

Now: The store returns the exact active dispatch, and close names it plus whether boundary, owner or requester differs.

#### Close notes

Was: The close note merge assumed append-only notes, and a note written in the pre-write gap refused the close.

Now: Rewritten notes are preserved and a note-only race retries.

#### Close proof scan

Was: Proof-input checks ran git per directory level and per candidate file and knew only the shortest ../ include spelling.

Now: One delivered-tree scan per commit is shared across worktrees; the batched source read streams so large trees cannot deadlock, and any ../ spelling resolves.

### Terminal and tooling

#### Compact-owner resizing

Was: The 3.78 no-op resize skip also skipped needed layouts when a Compact client owned input.

Now: Full viewer resizes under a Compact owner and same-size takeovers to or from Compact reschedule the layout.

#### terminal-qa carriage returns

Was: CR redraws and CRCRLF line ends dropped visible row text while reporting PASS.

Now: CR resets the cursor and overwrites cells in place, and erase-in-line controls apply.

#### Review checkouts and socket logs

Was: Symlinked CAS roots hid managed review checkouts, and macOS socket retention was unexplained.

Now: Roots are canonicalized and each kept socket path logs its reason at debug level.
