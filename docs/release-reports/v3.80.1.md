## What you can do now

### Release changes

#### Terminal checks on Mac

Was: In 3.80.0, the terminal check could approve captures with no visible output or append a stray `D` to a one-character first line.

Now: It reports the empty capture as a failure again and keeps that first line from gaining the extra `D`.


## Under the hood

### Terminal and tooling

#### BSD TTY captures

Was: In 3.80.0, terminal-qa retained BSD script's leading ^D and two backspaces, so empty TTY output could pass and a one-character first line could gain a stray D.

Now: On macOS/BSD, runInPty strips one exact leading ^D plus two backspaces before analysis. parseAnsi keeps its cursor-moving backspace behavior.
