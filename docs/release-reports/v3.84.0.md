## What you can do now

### Your accounts in one place

#### One list

Was: There was no single view of your subscriptions and their usage.

Now: `cas accounts list` shows every Claude and Codex account by email, with its plan, usage, reset times and any problem such as a duplicate or a login that no longer works.

#### Names you recognise

Was: The pickers showed folder names like `main` and `alt`, and two folders with one login looked unrelated.

Now: Rows show the email and folder name, and the original is listed before its copies. Your old names still work.

#### A recommended account

Was: You had to remember which account still had room.

Now: The account that resets soonest with room left is marked "recommended" with its reason, and the cursor starts on it. You can still pick any account, and nothing starts until you press Enter. Commander's New session sheet does the same.

#### Reset times you can plan around

Was: Usage showed only a percentage or "in 6h".

Now: Everywhere usage appears, it reads like "resets Tue 14:00 (in 6h)" in your local time, with the date for later days.

#### Tidy up

Was: A mistyped or retired account folder could not be removed.

Now: `cas accounts remove`, `rename`, `disable` and `enable` manage them. Your main account and accounts in use are protected, and removing asks first.

#### Clean new logins

Was: A cancelled or failed login left an empty folder that looked like an account.

Now: A new login only becomes an account when it succeeds.

#### Work keeps going

Was: A background session that hit its usage limit stopped until someone moved it by hand.

Now: It restarts once on the next recommended account and carries on with its task. If none has room, you are told when one will.

#### Mac usage

Was: Claude usage on a Mac could show nothing.

Now: It reads the login from the Mac Keychain.

### After installing

#### Restore shared settings

Was: Account folders recreated after linking lost their shared settings.

Now: Run `cas doctor --fix` once after installing to restore them.

#### Removal is final

Was: There was no way to remove an account.

Now: Removing one deletes it after you confirm; there is no backup copy.

#### Backup folder names

Was: An account folder whose name ended in a word like backup or old was hidden.

Now: It counts as an account. To keep a backup hidden, end its folder name with .backup after the account name.


## Under the hood

### Account registry

#### Shared read model

Was: Claude and Codex pickers each scanned their own config folders separately.

Now: One registry returns provider, folder, email, plan, login state, structured usage windows and flags (`duplicate_of`, `never_logged_in`, `stale_login`, `disabled`) under one 4-second deadline. Identity is a typed object.

#### Name resolution

Was: An unknown launch name created a new folder.

Now: Launch and spawn resolve aliases, emails and symlinked folders through the registry. Unknown names are refused with the login command, and ambiguous emails list their aliases.

#### Selection policy

Was: There was no shared recommendation.

Now: One selection function honours `accounts.selection` and `accounts.threshold` (default 90); idle windows count as unused, reset-unknown ones rank last, and excluded accounts carry reasons. Pickers, Commander and `spawn_workers` without `config_dir` use it.

#### Lifecycle

Was: Account folders had no lifecycle commands.

Now: `cas accounts remove|rename|disable|enable`. In-use checks read live process environments and registered agents, including on older Cassy databases. Logins are staged in a hidden temp folder, symlink aliases follow their physical account, and `accounts.disabled` stores the canonical path.

#### Limit recovery

Was: Limit evidence produced no account switch.

Now: One durable episode per limit queues one switch to the next recommended account and keeps the worktree, branch and task. A failed switch still sends one fallback notice naming the error. The "no eligible account until" time is the earliest real eligibility. The supervisor is never switched.

#### Reset display

Was: Reset text was relative or locale-dependent.

Now: One `format_reset` gives local `resets Tue 14:00 (in 6h)` and `(in 1d 23h)`, and `--compact` stays one line with `+N more`. `cas limits --json` `reset_at` is now a zoned RFC 3339 timestamp.

#### macOS Keychain

Was: Claude usage read only the credentials file in the account folder.

Now: It reads the account's Keychain item first, keyed by the NFC-hashed config selector, and never prints it.

#### Doctor and launch links

Was: Link drift after `cas accounts link` went unnoticed.

Now: `cas doctor` reports account link drift, `--fix` repairs it with a backup, and launches restore the chosen account's shared links.

#### Tooling

Was: Commander browser tests ran on Playwright 1.63.

Now: They run on Playwright 1.64.1.
