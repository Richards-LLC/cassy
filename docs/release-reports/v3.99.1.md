## What you can do now

### Release changes

#### Resume after a crash

Was: a crash could leave you unable to restart under the same name.

Now: a crashed session can be restarted under its own name, keeping its work and any unsaved commits.


## Under the hood

### Release changes

#### Same-name recovery

Was: retained PTY panes blocked relaunch, and recycle rejected stopped workers.

Now: exited panes are replaced while the same checkout, task branch and unpushed HEAD survive recovery.

#### Protect live sessions

Was: registry-only recovery could target a running replacement or cross session boundaries.

Now: queued recovery cannot stop a live same-name pane, and dead-row recovery requires an owning session.
