## What you can do now

### Fewer manual steps, fewer silent gaps

#### Releases close themselves

Was: after a release, each shipped task had to be closed by hand because changelog and map files had moved.

Now: closing follows the release history, so shipped work closes without overrides.

#### You can lift a release freeze

Was: Lift freeze asked for an unrelated fleet-control permission, and turning it on didn't help.

Now: your normal pairing can freeze or lift a release from the release card.

#### Missing history shows up

Was: when Commander asked for a conversation's history and got no answer, the gap stayed silent.

Now: after 15 seconds you see an error with a Retry button, for each page separately.

#### Undelivered decisions are noticed

Was: a decision meant for you could expire without anyone knowing it never reached a device.

Now: Cassy notices the miss, and delivery keeps retrying.

#### Approvals from your other machine are acted on

Was: a landing approval from your other machine could sit quietly for a while.

Now: it's picked up right away.

#### Parallel fixes merge cleanly

Was: two fixes that each added a QA entry collided and had to be redone.

Now: separate entries merge on their own; edits to the same entry still ask for a person.

#### Easier debugging and publishing

Was: a helper that failed to start vanished without a trace, and reports inside a helper's folder could not be published.

Now: failed starts keep their boot output, and those reports publish directly with secrets still protected.


## Under the hood

### Release changes

#### Release close

Was: closes after a release hit DELIVERY CONTENT DROPPED for `changelog.d/<id>.md` and CODEMAP lines rewritten during integration.

Now: close follows the full history to the promoting release commit and the final regenerated blob, with no reviewed-drop override.

#### Peer approvals

Was: an admitted peer premerge approval was refused after later plan bookkeeping changed the digest, and published landing approvals were treated as quiet receipts.

Now: the approval survives when task, delivery SHA, target and landed tree still match, and published landings wake idle supervisors.

#### Release freeze scope

Was: `lift_train_freeze` required factory-operate, which the pairing toggle could not grant.

Now: freeze and lift use machine-read pairing scope; revoked and preview credentials still cannot lift.

#### Commander history

Was: an unanswered history request left a silent partial transcript, and one late reply could clear another page's timeout.

Now: each request tracks its own 15-second deadline, error and Retry; older-page cursors retry independently.

#### Operator delivery

Was: an expiring transcript mirror copy with no receiving device produced no signal.

Now: the current supervisor is notified, including decisions carried in status replies; durable delivery keeps retrying.

#### Merging and publishing

Was: the QA journey registry conflicted on every pair of additive deliveries, and `artifact publish` refused files in registered worktrees under `.cas`.

Now: a registered merge driver unions disjoint bug families and keeps the newer date, and nested registered checkouts publish while Git internals, sockets and credentials stay refused.

#### Boot diagnostics

Was: a missing SessionStart canary lost the startup output with the pane.

Now: bounded, redacted boot output is retained and findable by worker or spawn request.
