## What you can do now

### Smaller databases, faster writes

#### Conflict log stays small

Was: every sync conflict kept another full copy forever; one project reached 22 GB.

Now: each task keeps its ten most recent copies.

#### Old records are cleaned up

Was: ended sessions and delivered messages were kept forever.

Now: the regular cleanup removes them once nothing needs them; pending ones are always kept.

#### Opening tasks is fast

Was: each time Cassy opened its task list it read every task in full, up to 2 seconds while everything else waited.

Now: a few milliseconds.

#### Reviews close cleanly

Was: a manual review on another machine could attach to the wrong commit, so the task could never close.

Now: it attaches to the task's own work.

#### Slow writes are named

Was: when writes waited minutes, nothing said what held them up.

Now: anything holding the writer over a second is logged by name.


## Under the hood

### Release changes

#### Retention

Was: `sync_conflicts`, `agent_session_ends` and the operator delivery outbox grew without bound.

Now: 10 conflict rows per entity plus the newest per strategy; superseded session ends and completed outbox rows pruned by the retention pass.

#### TaskStore::init

Was: DDL plus a delivery-cycle check filtering `status` row by row on every uncached open (141 ms warm, 1.8 s cold on a large store).

Now: DDL once per (file, schema_version); the check uses `idx_tasks_status` (4-7 ms).

#### StoreWorker WARN

Was: writer holders were invisible.

Now: jobs holding the writer 1 s or longer log label, elapsed_ms and queue depth.

#### Supervisor-direct verdicts

Was: `verification action=add` bound the supervisor's checkout HEAD, so G-65 refused close.

Now: it binds the dispatch's delivery head.
