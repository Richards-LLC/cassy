## What you can do now

### Coordinating sessions stay reachable

#### No more frozen session after a long day

Was: a coordinating session filled to about 967,000 tokens, Claude compacted it on its own, and Cassy's startup check then froze the pane for up to 12 minutes.

Now: it hands off at 80% of its real window, and startup waits at most 12 seconds.

#### Messages reach an idle session

Was: on a busy machine, a Commander message to an idle coordinating session could sit queued for minutes until someone typed in its pane.

Now: messages are delivered ahead of other background writes.

#### Verifiers start on a busy Mac

Was: on a loaded Mac, starting a task's reviewer could fail after four quick tries, and the close had to be retried by hand.

Now: a busy machine is waited for, with growing pauses.

#### Releases with finished notes tasks cut cleanly

Was: a release whose notes or report task had already closed could be refused as having nothing to ship.

Now: it counts as done and the release proceeds.


## Under the hood

### Release changes

#### SessionStart recovery

Was: delivery recovery ran inline on the shared SQLite connection plus per-delivery git checks; a compact on a busy project held the hook 6 to 12 minutes.

Now: it runs off-thread on thread-private connections, 10 s budget plus 2 s grace.

#### Recovery timeout

Was: no timeout existed.

Now: a timeout is shown inline and retried on the next startup; unchecked deliveries are named with their commit receipts.

#### Auto-clear threshold

Was: Claude transcripts carry no context window, so the clear waited for a fixed token cap and Claude auto-compacted first.

Now: the window comes from Claude's status line and the handoff and clear start at 80% of it.

#### Idle-supervisor delivery

Was: transport claims were dropped when they landed after the 10 s inject timeout and re-queued behind the same write backlog.

Now: claims are kept until they land, and prompt-queue selection and claims run first on the store writer.

#### Verifier launch

Was: a daemon that missed the response deadline was treated as a failed launch (os error 35).

Now: it counts as contention; the request backs off for up to 15 minutes.

#### Release cut preview

Was: a closed no-code member counted as missing release membership.

Now: it counts as closed.
