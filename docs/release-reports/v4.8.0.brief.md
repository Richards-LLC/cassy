## Single idea

A published release now closes the work it shipped: 19 changes, 10 of them in how tasks close and get checked, published 202 s after main.

## Hero form

Verdict plus a unit-dot plot of shipped changes by area. Tasks is the largest area (10 of 19 dots, in the verdict color): the release is mostly about deliveries closing correctly and the checks that gate them, which is the verdict's claim. Areas were regrouped by hand from each CHANGELOG heading (automatic title-word placement put release and verifier changes under Commander and Sessions); the generator's four areas and their order are kept. Counts changes, not effort. No zero rows on page one.

## Emotional register

Assured, humane, collected: regular serif verdict, sans text, mono folio, quiet rules, on the DESIGN.md surfaces (gradient hero, lavender cards; dark scheme on screen only).

## Distinctive move

The change map keeps its generator identity; a six-dot row returns beside Install. This edition's Tasks row wraps past eight dots, so its weight reads before the numbers do; Releases (5) carries the reconcile work that names the release.

## Deliberately omitted

No KPI cards before the argument, logo band, network assets or invented metrics. Missing: per-stage timings (receipt has no STAGE_SECS), request-to-published, cut-to-published, lesson recurrence; no GitHub issues referenced, so the fixes ledger is header-only. Cannot support any claim about stage speed or about train speed: first commit to main took 90712 s against an 1800 s budget, shown on the glance strip as over budget. Known limit stated in Under the hood and Limits: reconcile can print CLOSE for a child its own close gate holds; fix ships next release.
