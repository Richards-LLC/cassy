## What you can do now

### Commander stays in sync, and cleanup is tidier

#### Answers keep reaching Commander

Was: after `/clear` and a reload of Cassy's tools, later answers silently stopped showing up in Commander.

Now: they keep arriving, once each.

#### Finished commands clear themselves

Was: a finished command such as Reload Cassy left a result card that needed Dismiss.

Now: a short message appears and fades on its own. Failed or unconfirmed commands keep their card.

#### Project card names are readable

Was: the first letters of a name hid under the avatar, and long names ran past the edge of phone-width cards.

Now: the name starts after the avatar and fits; the machine name gives way first.

#### A stuck Cancel recovers

Was: a refused or timed-out Cancel left the command stuck at queued and blocked every later command.

Now: Cassy keeps following the command to its real outcome, and later commands work.

#### Finish cleans up after itself

Was: Finish could leave background shells and servers it started still running.

Now: it stops its own background work and checks that none is left.

#### Releases start themselves when ready

Was: a release that passed every check waited for someone to start it.

Now: Cassy starts it once every check is green and the notes are ready.

#### Mac Claude logins stay put

Was: account linking could move a Claude login kept in the macOS Keychain and report it as drift.

Now: active logins are left in place.

#### Search and memories

Was: tasks made on another machine could be missing from search, and renaming a memory by title alone did not save.

Now: search reads the current task list and stops exact duplicates, and title-only renames save.


## Under the hood

### Release changes

#### Commander mirror

Was: `/mcp` reconnect after `/clear` restored the first pane's session id, and a second registration for one pane double-mirrored.

Now: the rebound session survives reconnect, and one transcript mirrors once.

#### Commander UI

Was: finished commands left a result card, a refused Cancel froze the receipt at queued, and project-card names clipped or overflowed.

Now: success shows a self-clearing toast, Cancel resumes outcome polling, and the who-line sizes to the avatar and ellipsizes in narrow cards.

#### Auto-cut

Was: a green train waited for a manual `scripts/release.sh`.

Now: an exact train tip with a green full-suite receipt, changelog fragments and a notes draft freezes and cuts itself. Backfill skips frozen successor trains.

#### Close gates

Was: first park could read an earlier failing-test commit, refusals hid paths and revisions, and filenames counted as proof claims.

Now: first park reads the recorded pushed tip, refusals name the path, gate and checked revision, and local summaries are not external proof.

#### Delivery identity

Was: historical branches or lane reuse could displace the current delivery, and shared upkeep invalidated sibling reviews.

Now: close follows the recorded parked branch, recovery keeps the current receipt, and generated CODEMAP or overview upkeep keeps approvals.

#### Verification

Was: a crashed automatic verifier held its claim, a stale agent row blocked verdicts, and a target-only move re-asked an answered call.

Now: one retry replaces a crashed verifier, process liveness decides "running", and a target-only re-dispatch reuses the answer.

#### Task start and moves

Was: an epic move kept the old lane target, an empty retargeted branch could not be rebound, and linked-checkout WIP lost ownership.

Now: matching targets follow the move, a clean empty replacement branch can be approved, and linked WIP keeps its recorded owner.

#### Tasks and queues

Was: dep_remove echoed the removed edge, `duplicate` deps acted as blocking, and startup assignments could reorder.

Now: the receipt names the removed edge, unsupported types are refused, and startup queues keep order with Current and Held shown apart.

#### Spawn and accounts

Was: lane models were resolved before the account was chosen, and Keychain Claude logins looked like link drift.

Now: models resolve per selected account with its registered fallback, and native Claude auth paths are preserved.

#### Notices and handoffs

Was: capacity waits woke the supervisor per worker, cached reads tripped auto-clear, and handoffs crossed machines.

Now: disk waits share one notice, auto-clear measures the latest call, and handoffs stay within machine, project and role.
