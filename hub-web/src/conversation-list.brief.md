# Brief: grouped conversation list

## Single idea
A project's conversations share one bounded surface, ending before the next project begins.

## Hero form
Nested list: a project-and-machine heading and its session rows sit inside one outlined surface, making membership visible without reading every row.

## Emotional register
Calm, legible — the Glass raised surface and strong neutral outline establish a group without competing with violet selection or unread marks.

## Distinctive move
The shared outline closes below the last session's controls; the next singleton returns to the surrounding glass.

## Deliberately omitted
No collapse control or extra tab stop: every conversation stays visible and keeps its existing keyboard order. No status colour for the group boundary, since status belongs to the session.

Design: hub-web/DESIGN.md@ce0d8adc9; existing Glass tokens, Inter and mono codenames; shell brand remains unchanged.
